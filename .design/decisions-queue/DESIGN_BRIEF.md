# Decisions queue

Reconstructed from the owner direction in this task. It is the baseline for the review, not a new interview.

## Intent

The Ward page Decisions tab is a compact queue for synthetic bed-flow decisions: Staffing, Intake, Departures and Leave. It is not clinical decision support.

## Aesthetic

Apple-like precision. Frosted, nested modules. Moderate curves, not pills on rows. Dense, not sparse. No full-width empty rows.

Colour is a crisp signal: a thin group rail, a coloured icon, a small status chip, and a slate action button. Panel fills stay neutral. Titles stay ink. No red wash across a card, and no near-black toolbar.

## Type and controls

Two sizes only: 13px for titles and names, 12px for times, facts, chips and buttons. Actions stay about 26px tall. The filter is a light segmented control.

## Behaviour that must stay

Live records drive the rows. The tab badge matches the queue due count. Empty groups say “None waiting” or “None out”. The unwired sentence appears only on the true stub. The census form, flag-a-bed form, pipeline, leave form and activity log stay off this tab.
