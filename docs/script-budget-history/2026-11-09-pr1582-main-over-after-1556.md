# 9 Nov 2026, main went 0.1 KB over on three screens after #1555 and #1556 (PR #1582)

Raised movement 593.0 to 593.5, bed meeting 572.0 to 572.5 and statistics 611.9 to 612.5
(0.5 to 0.6 KB each).

Why: every pull request was inside its limits on its own, but after #1562, #1555 and #1556 merged
in a row, main's own CI build (run 42654163717, commit `a6740f02`) measured three screens just over:

| Route       | Main measured | Old limit | New limit |
| ----------- | ------------- | --------- | --------- |
| movement    | 593.1         | 593.0     | 593.5     |
| bed meeting | 572.0         | 572.0     | 572.5     |
| statistics  | 612.0         | 611.9     | 612.5     |

Diagnosis: #1555 only reworded code comments (98 source files, equal lines in and out), which the
minifier strips. The shipped growth is #1556, Josh's signed D-106 clinical values: it adds values and
their notes to `ward-clinical-values.ts` and `ward-clinical-value-notes.ts`, which nearly every screen
imports, plus small edits in `ward-model.ts` and the derivations. Trim tried: none is safe here. The
new code is signed clinical content in shared modules, so it cannot load on demand or shrink without
changing what Josh signed, and the overage (0.1 KB or less) is smaller than the about 0.4 KB that any
new on-demand chunk adds to every route.

This restores main to green with about 0.4 KB of room on each. Pull requests that grow
these screens still carry their own raise (budget-gate.mjs judges each one against main).
