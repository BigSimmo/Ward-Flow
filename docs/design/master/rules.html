Look (owner rulings)

  • Tokens only, never hex. Every colour, size and radius comes from src/app/ward-flow-v9-tokens.css, which the Tokens tab lists.
  • No coloured side edges. No left or right stripe, inset bar or tone-coloured border on any row, card, tile or stat box ("it feels tacky", 31 Oct). Show tone with the StatusGlyph or a small tinted tag. A selected item gets the selected tint with a neutral border. This is enforced by tests/wf-no-side-stripes.test.ts.
  • The minimum text size is 12px, with no exception for uppercase text and none for column heads.
  • Colour lives in glyphs and the hero. Red means act now: those items take the act tint and their glyph, never an edge.
  • Figures are in mono with tabular digits. Chart axes are in Geist. Strip figures are 15px, with 20px used only in one focal strip per page (Ward shipped its 20px figures).
  • Rows are 44px by default, 36px dense and 52px for touch. The T1 pill is neutral. Closed things are slate.
  • Glass is about 80% opaque with the sheen kept. There is one action blue and a lit hero. Bright light is a setting.
  • The phone is its own design, not a squashed desktop. Every page has a focal part, a part of its own and a page-specific hero.

Controls

  • No 48px controls. A phone button is drawn at 40px, small ones too (Q295), with an invisible cover that keeps the 48px tap area. Phone tabs are 40px on a 48px track (Q225). Desktop segmented and pill tabs are 30px at 13px. Desktop buttons are 36px (--wf-h-md); 28px (--wf-target) is the smallest target.
  • The one exception: Accept, Pull and Decline (data-placement-action) stay a full 48px on phones.
  • Dates are typed in the kit's DD/MM/YYYY DateField, never the browser's date picker. Use TimePresets, SearchField and ShowMoreRow from the kit, never a page's own copy.
  • One filled button per row or panel. The rest are quiet.
  • A control a desk can never use is not shown. A control that is unavailable for now stays, with aria-disabled and its reason (disabledReason).
  • An unconnected control says exactly "Not wired in this prototype." (D4).
  • Leaving with unsaved work uses requestConfirm: "Leave this page?", with "Leave without saving" and "Keep editing", and focus starts on Keep editing. Never use the browser's confirm, alert or prompt.
  • Toasts sit --wf-toast-dock-gap above the phone dock.

Pages

  • Restructure, not restyle. Ask what each part's job is. Each fact lives in one right place.
  • Keep a slim header strip in a full-height band: the title, one strip of figures and at most one on-hero action. Never shrink the band itself.
  • A list-and-panel page opens on the first useful item, never empty. Home's Placement panel is the exception.
  • Show about eight rows, then "Show 8 more". Never use a small box that scrolls inside a panel.
  • Group repeats into one row with one action, such as "Chase all".
  • One design for every desk. Role versions differ only in what that desk may see or do.
  • Filter and highlight chips sit in one row, and a chip with nobody in it is hidden. Phone chip rows put the count first and slide sideways with an edge fade.
  • Checklists have one head line with the counts, a bulk action and Hide, then a grid of one-line cards, three across, showing the first nine.
  • Less is more. Add a mark, tag or tab only when it answers a question the page can't already answer.
  • The drawer is headed "Alerts", not "Tasks". Pinned sidebar pages last for the open browser tab only.

Pop-ups and side panels

  • The anatomy: the name, then a small status tag beside it, then one quiet context line. The close button takes focus. There is no second Back link.
  • Open on a short Summary first. Long lists go behind tabs inside the panel, each tab with its count.
  • Facts are label and value pairs in short titled groups, three to five per group, never repeating the header.
  • Blocked, escalated and clearance notes sit together in one compact list with icons.
  • Actions are pinned at the foot, kit-sized, with one filled.
  • Documents are chips that open a clearly marked synthetic preview.
  • Forms open blank. Nothing is pre-selected: no day, no legal answer, no ward.

Graphs, grids and beds

  • Heat-map cells show the number alone, with the shade deepening as it grows. An empty day is a faint dot. Days after tomorrow carry the shared "may move" hatch. Day heads are two lines, with today in accent blue.
  • Bed pictures read "1 ready of 20 beds", with a bed-type tag, the squares on one line, then a note line only when there is something to note.
  • Each record has one timeline: a single rail in time order with a live Now point and the due time. History folds underneath.
  • Draw charts to scale and only from existing figures. Never add calculations for a mock-up. Weekly figures are illustrative.

Words and safety

  • Synthetic data only. No patient names or typed text in URLs, logs, screenshots, commits or memory.
  • Patient names are surname first as recorded, in full, with the UMRN; staff names are as written ("Dr S. Chen", Q226). Both wrap rather than cut ("Jenkinwood, Sarah"). Place names wrap too.
  • Times exactly as typed. Legal-form times are never a countdown, "due in" or "passed ago" (D-93). Never default a written time to now (Q120). Never compute or show Mental Health Act periods or section numbers (D5, D-70).
  • Fewer warnings. Keep a warning only where a rule or a real risk needs it, say it once, and keep it quiet. Safeguard wording stays word for word ("Not legally checked."). A missing fact reads "Not recorded" in ink-3.
  • Say a time once: "Sent 3 days ago · Due 2 days ago".
  • Never say something is not stored, private or anonymised unless a test proves it. No typed text goes in browser storage.
  • Line caps never change on-screen words. Split the file instead.

How to build a screen fast

  • Build only approved designs. A page changes its look only after Josh approves that page's mock-up (D-91). Team search is not approved.
  • Port, don't redraw. Lift the components and tokens from the screen's tab here (the same React the app uses) and apply them straight to the site. Compare against the approved patch, not an older static HTML.
  • Approved sources: Handover is handover-hifi.patch (not mock.html), Movements v4, ED r3, Transport is Josh's zip viewer (desk-book.html is an older draft), Patients is its zip, Statistics is statistics-hifi.patch, and Forms is the shipped main version (Q133).
  • Where a merged PR deliberately differs from its mock-up, follow what shipped.
  • Build with the kit in src/components/wf, and never copy a primitive into a screen.
  • Each page build removes its page's remaining coloured edges and lowers the side-stripe baseline.
  • New events go in EVENT_ROLE and its permissions test.
  • Phone script limits: a PR may raise one by up to 5 KB per screen after diagnosing the growth and trying a trim. More than that needs Josh's yes (D-82).

Lessons from the 1 Nov builds

  • Mock-ups take the shell from the master, never from main (Josh, 1 Nov). Copy the sidebar, header, phone dock, kit and tokens from the newest master screens (the redesign preview with ?shell=next), not from a running page on main: main's Alerts page still draws the pre-30 Oct header. Before posting, compare each desk with the master's Home and Ward pages at 390, 820, 1280 and 1440, in light and dark.
  • The band hero is the header. Every page uses the kit Hero with data-wf-hero="band", full width and joined to the dark app bar, with the light sheet below. The phone uses the same Hero, not the older card hero.
  • Use the kit's Badge, not one-off .tag, .chip or .pill CSS. This was the audit's most common miss.
  • Use the shared formatters (clk, dur, durMinutes, DateField). Patients re-implemented clk().
  • Check phone buttons: several builds drew 48px buttons that should be 40px with a cover.
  • knip fails on unused exports, so each PR carries only the parts its own tab uses (Statistics). Deleting replaced code under 30 days old needs Josh's yes (Q138, Q141).
  • Rewrite tests that pin old wording to the new wording, keeping their coverage. Never skip a test to get green.
  • Stacked PRs (Transport #1235 on #1221, Alerts #1233 on #1230) are not duplicates. Confirm with the thread before calling anything a duplicate.
  • The cloud clone is shallow. Deepening it (git fetch --deepen) is network Git and needs Josh's yes (Q139). Until then the sign-out and format guards treat the checkout as non-public, so run prettier directly.
  • The cloud has Node 22 but the app needs 24. Use /opt/node24/bin, or npm ci --engine-strict=false for mock-up builds. Josh's PC session does the local renders.
  • Sign-out log: /mnt/project-files/ward-flow/signout/, with one claim file per branch. Only exact paths or folders ending in "/" count as a claim.
  • The old ARTIFACTS.md links don't open. Use mockups/LINKS.md and this master page.

Process

  • Ward Flow repo only. Check that origin names BigSimmo/Ward-Flow before any Git write.
  • Ask before any provider, live service or network Git. Stage named files only. Never use --no-verify.
  • Claim files on issue #688 and in the sign-out log before editing.
  • Auto-merge counts as Josh's yes. Never merge by hand, and never turn auto-merge on or off yourself (#1230 stays off until its bug is fixed).
  • Hosted CI is the full gate, so don't run duplicate test suites. Open PRs as drafts and take them out of draft once CI is green and the page matches its mock-up.
  • Merging is not going live. The live site follows the live branch through the 6 am Perth release, or a manual run when Josh says "release now".
  • Check each mock-up against this page before posting it: side edges, 40px phone buttons, one on-hero action, eight rows then "Show 8 more", and kit glyphs, buttons and Badge.
  • Q134: keep outside commits that match the mock-up, and revert the ones that don't with revert commits.
  • Keep one handover file per screen in /mnt/project-files/ward-flow/handover/ and update it when you stop.

Talking to Josh

  • Plain, warm Australian English. Give the answer first, in short sentences.
  • Say what is verified, what isn't, and any action he must take.
  • Number questions, give one recommendation each, and log each one in HANDOVER.md, which is the source of truth for the next free Q number.
  • Keep tokens ultra-light. Post only finished links and real questions.
  • Leave clinical, privacy and product decisions to Josh.
