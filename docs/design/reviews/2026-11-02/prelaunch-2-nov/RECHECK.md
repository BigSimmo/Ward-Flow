## New issues

### N1. Portrait iPads (744 to 768) get a broken mix of phone and tablet layouts

- Page: every page. Worst on Ward, Home and Waiting.
- Width: 744 to 768 (iPad mini and iPad portrait), light and dark. 743 and 820 look right.
- What's wrong: the phone dock and phone menu show, but the top bar is 73px tall, not 56 (phone) or 60 (tablet). The page title is pushed against the top edge and clipped. The clock chip wraps under "Statewide". Hero figures use tablet boxes inside the phone shell, and the Ward hero boxes are cut off at the bottom. Phone segment rules (48rem) and page overrides (now 46.4375rem) disagree, so control sizes differ from both designs.
- Cause: #1291 moved most stylesheets to 743/744, but the JS phone hook still uses 48rem (use-is-phone.ts:6). The shell bar does too (ward-bar.module.css:146, 186, 282, 604, and its `@container ward-bar` 768px rule). #1291's own notes say not to release without part 2.
- Evidence: evidence/R-1-ward-768.jpg, evidence/R-1-home-768.jpg, evidence/R-1-waiting-744-dark.jpg, and evidence/R-1-home-743-for-comparison.jpg.
- Recommend: fix #1298's merge conflicts and merge it before the next 6 am release, then check 744, 768 and 820 again. If it can't make that release, hold the release or revert #1291.
