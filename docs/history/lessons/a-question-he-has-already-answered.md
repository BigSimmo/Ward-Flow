**2026-09-18.** I drafted a review document pairing Ward Flow's 64 community team names with real WA
services, and asked Josh to decide six rows. **Four of them he had already ruled on, on 2026-09-05**,
recorded in `community-ratified-aliases.ts` — the four `Inner City` spellings, ruled one service,
with his question, his answer and the suburb counts he was shown all sitting in the table.

🔴 **HIS ANSWER TODAY MATCHED HIS ANSWER THEN, AND THAT IS PRECISELY WHY IT GOES UNNOTICED.** A
re-answered question and a newly-answered one are indistinguishable in the outcome: question asked,
answer returned, answer correct, work proceeds. **Had the two answers disagreed, it would have been
investigated within a minute.** The agreement is what buries it.

⚠️ **The cost lands entirely on him and leaves no artefact.** No gate fails, no test reddens, no
count moves. The only evidence is his time, which nothing in this process measures.

## How to apply

- ✅ **Treat "is this actually open?" as a verification step held to the same standard as any other
  claim.** Before drafting a question: grep the ratified/decisions tables, the owner-answers
  documents, and the relevant guards' expectation lines **for the exact subject**.
- ✅ **When several questions go at once, check each separately.** They were opened at different
  times by different work; one being genuinely open says nothing about its neighbours.
- ✅ **State in the message where you looked.** The next person then inherits the search instead of
  repeating it — and if you looked in the wrong place, that is visible too.
- ⚠️ **In Ward Flow specifically, the places are:** `community-ratified-aliases.ts`,
  `docs/ward-flow/owner-decisions-*.md`, `docs/ward-flow/owner-answers-*.md`, and the task ledger.

## Its sibling, and neither covers the other

[[prove-the-task-is-still-outstanding]] is this failure applied to assigned **work** — an assignment
is a statement about the past, not evidence the work is outstanding. **Same shape, different
object.** That one costs an agent's effort and is caught eventually by the work already existing;
this one costs the owner's attention and is caught by nothing.
