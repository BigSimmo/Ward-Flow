**2026-09-04, asked directly and answered directly.** Ward Flow's destination is **"a real tool — to
be used": software a bed coordinator actually opens on a weekday morning**, replacing a whiteboard, a
spreadsheet or a phone round. Not a pitch to persuade funders, not a specification to think with, not
a private aid to the owner's own understanding — all three were offered as alternatives and rejected.

## What that changes, and it is most of the project's assumptions

- **Synthetic data is a STAGE, not the destination.** Every "this is a synthetic prototype" banner is
  scaffolding with an end date, not a permanent property.
- 🔴 **"The app has no notion of who is looking" becomes a BLOCKER, not a simplification.** Today a
  role is only what a decision is _recorded against_ — never a person — and no function takes a
  viewer, scope or role argument (measured at the master line, 2026-09-04). A real tool needs
  identity, permissions and an audit trail; a demonstration does not.
- **Clinical and information governance attach.** Real patient data in WA means privacy obligations,
  approval to hold it, and a custodian — none of which a synthetic prototype needs.
- **Integration becomes a question that must be answered** rather than deferred.
- **"Not a medical device" needs re-examining** against what it actually becomes, rather than being
  carried forward as a banner.

⚠️ **Do not soften this into "eventually, maybe".** The owner picked the heaviest of four options
knowing the implications were listed against each.

## How this was established, which matters for trusting it

Asked as one question with four fully-described options and their consequences spelled out, at the
start of a deep review — not inferred from enthusiasm, not read off a document. The three rejected
options are recorded here because a later reader is more likely to drift toward "it's a prototype"
than toward the truth.

## Where the record lives — answered 2026-09-04, and it is NOT a clean either/or

Asked whether Ward Flow owns the record or only views one. The owner's answer sits **between** the
two options offered, and the middle is the design:

> "It is largely everything from example 1 … however, it is then also used to record and communicate
> bed decisions quickly. There is bulky hospital software … however this is lightweight and made for
> being able to rapidly fill in and update to inform all the bedflow decision. This means it is where
> bedflow decisions are communicated on but it is also kept in another bulky hospital DMR ledger."

So:

|                            |                                                                                                                                                                        |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Ward Flow owns**         | the search and the decisions around it — who needs a bed, who was asked, who declined, who accepted, who is in transit. **And the COMMUNICATION of those decisions.**  |
| **The hospital DMR holds** | the formal, bulky record. The same facts land there too.                                                                                                               |
| **The design constraint**  | ⚠️ **LIGHTWEIGHT AND RAPID IS THE POINT.** It exists because the hospital software is bulky. Anything that makes it slower to fill in attacks its reason for existing. |

🔴 **"COMMUNICATE" IS LOAD-BEARING AND THE APP HAS NO MECHANISM FOR IT.** There is no notification of
any kind anywhere in the application, while several owner rulings already require one ("the referrer
must be told when a pull is cancelled"). The stated purpose and the built reality diverge here more
than anywhere else.

**And duplicate entry into the DMR is accepted, not a defect to design away** — the two systems hold
the same facts on purpose. Do not propose integration as though double entry were the problem; the
owner has already priced it.
