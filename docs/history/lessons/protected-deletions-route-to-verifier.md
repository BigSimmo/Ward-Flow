**Owner ruling, 2026-09-12:** _"I approved it, but in the future have decisions like this sent to
you first automatically."_

**Before any deletion or move of protected work, the deciding chat sends it to Ward Verifier
first — automatically, not as a courtesy and not only when unsure.** Scope is the `CLAUDE.md`
protected list and nothing wider: anything matching `ward-flow` / `ward-management` / `ward-board`,
any handover or decision document **including superseded ones**, any git worktree, either unpushed
ward branch, the memory store, the backups.

🔴 **THE CASE THAT PROMPTED IT IS THE ONE MOST EASILY MISSED: an approval the owner gave in ANOTHER
chat.** A Ward Flow screen was deleted; the chat that did it had a real, correctly obtained
approval and had backed up first. ⚠️ **It was still unverifiable from the Verifier chat — and
"the owner approved it" arrives already carrying its own authority, which is precisely when nobody
checks.** ✅ **So the routing applies to a deletion the deciding chat BELIEVES is approved, not only
to one it is unsure about.**

**Why:** the owner talks to several chats at once. An approval given in one is invisible in the
others, and the act it authorises is irreversible.

✅ **What the check IS:** confirm the approval exists, say what would be lost, confirm a backup.
🔴 **What it is NOT:** a veto, or a second chat disagreeing with him. **The owner's word stands.**
**It must not become a bottleneck** — this is a visibility rule, not an approval gate.
