# One question, two chats, and the looser standard went to the person who cannot check it

**2026-09-10.** Ward Lead and Ward Builder Four put the same decision to the owner within the same
hour, framed differently, neither knowing the other had. **Both recommended the same answer**, which
is the only reason this is recoverable and not a mess. It is written up because the answer agreeing
is luck, and the three defects underneath it are not.

---

## 1 · The known failure, walked into by someone who had it in memory

_"Two chats asking the owner about one decision get two honest and different answers"_ is already a
recorded rule here, and the question shapes the answer. Four holds that memory and **said so in the
same message that announced they had done it**. Knowing a failure mode does not stop you performing
it; only a routing rule does. **Owner questions now route through Ward Lead** — send the framing,
not the question.

---

## 2 · 🔴 The second framing offered work that the first framing's measurement rules out

This is the part that could have cost real effort. Four's question closed:

> _"The alternative is that I take that on next: find a way to separate a disclosure sentence from a
> safety sentence, so the rule can cover all 51 screens that disclose. Want me to start on extending
> it to the other screens, or leave it?"_

A reasonable-sounding second option. **But the measurement of the actual proposal says it fails for
a structural reason, not a cleverness reason:** a governance banner's first paragraph is where this
codebase puts its **safety** statement, and the provenance statement sits elsewhere in the banner or
is the badge itself. Measured: **35 blocks reached, 42 of 63 sentences reddened, almost all correct
prose.**

**So "yes, go" to the second framing commissions work the first framing had already priced as
not-working-as-posed.** Two honest recommendations that agree can still contain one live option that
should not be offered.

---

## 3 · The reason given was a number about a different rule

Four's framing justified "leave it" with **44 of 58 banner sentences flagged**. That figure measures
a rule which reads EVERY sentence in a banner. The option on the table keeps the paragraph anchoring
and widens only the trigger. **Right conclusion, wrong evidence — and the evidence was spent on the
owner rather than on a peer who could have checked it.**

---

## 4 · ⚠️ The discipline held in the artefact and was dropped in the sentence to the owner

Four measured **45** disclosing files; Ward Lead measured **51**. Different readings of "discloses",
nothing rests on it. **Four deliberately recorded both as unreconciled in the test file** — the right
call, and better than picking one nobody can re-derive.

**An hour later they quoted the other chat's 51 to the owner as a bare fact.**

Nobody was careless, and the direction is the whole finding:

> **The strict standard was applied to the reader who can check it, and the loose one to the reader
> who cannot.** A file is re-read by someone who can open the code and disagree. The owner is not an
> engineer and has no way to test a number in a sentence. **He needs the caveat MORE than the file
> does, and he got it less.**

**Rule: a figure that is unreconciled in the artefact is unreconciled in the summary.** If it is not
worth a clause to the owner, it was not worth stating.

---

## What was done

Four told the owner directly that he is holding one question from two chats, which one carries the
measured version, and that he should answer that one — **without re-asking, re-framing, or adding a
third recommendation.** Adding a correction rather than a new question is what kept this at two
versions instead of three.
