What this page is

  • A single ward’s own statistics, reached from that ward’s home screen. Nothing on it aggregates across wards, and nothing on it writes anything back — it is read-only.
  • SCGH Adult Open’s identity — Sir Charles Gairdner Hospital, adult, 24 beds, all open, no locked bay — is taken from the ward switcher on the approved mockup-ward-home-v4.html prototype, so the two do not contradict each other if the owner opens both. Nothing else on this page is drawn from that or any other fixture: every statistic below is invented for this prototype alone.

Vocabulary this page uses on purpose

  • Ready is the smaller of Allocatable and Empty (owner ruling R-B-09) — never any of this page’s retired wording for that figure. If Allocatable itself were zero, the correct word for that limit alone would be “no free bed,” not “Ready” — that case does not arise in today’s figures, where Allocatable is 1.
  • The third bed stage is discharged, never “released.”
  • This ward reports 0 beds on leave today — a real, measured zero. A bed on leave is information, never availability (owner ruling R11), and is never folded into Empty or Ready.

What is invented

  • Every figure on this page — the bed split (including Allocatable and Empty), the 30 days of Occupied and Ready counts behind the centrepiece chart, all 21 lengths of stay plotted as individual dots and their average and median, every admission and discharge count and the 7 daily counts behind the two sparklines, the 9-patient discharge pipeline, its ring share and its reasons, all referral counts including the no-free-bed/not-suitable split, the sex mix, the age band, the one-to-one nursing capacity and its staleness, and the three long-stay references and their day counts.
  • This ward’s current time, “confirmed 21:48,” and the reporting window, “last 30 days ending today.”

That is different from a zero: a zero here means the count was taken and came back none; this means the count was never possible to take.

One-to-one nursing capacity has not been reconfirmed since this morning’s handover. This page states that fact and shows the figure as Unknown rather than guessing forward from a stale number (owner ruling R16).

Not a caseload, and not a waiting list

  • “Beds expected to free” lists the reason recorded today for each. It is never a target for how many should be freed, and a lower number next week is not, by itself, evidence that anything changed for the better.
  • A zero anywhere on this page is a real, measured zero unless a caption beside it says the figure could not be taken. It is never used to mean “not tracked.”
