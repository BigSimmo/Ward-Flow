Why distance is a clinical cost

Every kilometre above is within greater Perth — close enough to read as inconvenience, not a clinical cost. For WACHS, that is not true: an export of hundreds of kilometres separates a patient from family, making them harder to visit, discharge and follow up.

What is real, taken from the repository

  • EMHS’s 4 hospitals, 7 wards, 105 beds and every Ready figure (min(allocatable, empty)) are read from ward-sites.ts, the same file and derivation the app itself uses.
  • Every figure in “The four health services, side by side” is read from that same file’s service field, counting only hospitals with at least one inpatient ward — why Joondalup and Peel don’t count, and WACHS shows 0 emergency departments.
  • EMHS’s 3 emergency departments are also read from ward-sites.ts; Bentley Health Service runs none of its own.
  • The three community team names are ward-catchment.ts’s own grouping. Midland’s and Bentley’s caseload figures are reused from the sibling overview screen.

What is invented

  • Every net-flow figure — 14 sent, 5 taken in, their daily running totals, the net export of 9, and the destination distances (~19 km, ~8 km). Only the 30-day totals are reused elsewhere; the daily distribution is invented for this chart.
  • Every referral-demand figure — 52 raised, 30 placed, 3 declined, 5 open, the 57.7% ring. The 14 sent is net flow’s same 14. The 3 declines are never split into a guessed “no bed” vs. “not suitable” count.
  • The 31-hour longest wait, and the per-department figures behind it, are reused from the sibling overview screen for the same synthetic day.
  • Inner City’s caseload figures (42, 3, 2) are invented fresh for this screen.
  • The liveness marker and the switcher’s “Net export” chip.

What this screen does not do

  • Compute a combined median wait across EMHS’s three EDs — it states the longest wait instead.
  • Show SMHS’s, NMHS’s or WACHS’s own worked figures — open the switcher for those.
  • Let a coordinator place a patient from this screen — a placement always goes back to the ward by phone.
  • Use a zero to mean “not tracked” — a zero here is always real and measured.
