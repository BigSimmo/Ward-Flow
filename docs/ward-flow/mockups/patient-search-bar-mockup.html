Architectural & Design Decisions Summary

1. Universal Curvature & Scale Shrunk from a clunky 42px height to a refined 33px with smooth 9px radius (--r-input). Matches the top universal search bar in the shell header with subtle inset shadow and crisp focus ring.
2. Gap Elimination & Layout Balance In Option 1 (Recommended), placing the search bar on the SAME horizontal row as the dropdown filters eliminates the awkward full-width cavern. Dropdowns and sort controls now sit in natural visual harmony.
3. 5 KPI Cards Placement Option 1 positions the 5 KPI cards in their natural overview layer directly beneath the header ribbon with crisp hairline borders, while Option 2 explores integrating them directly into the console deck as interactive tabs.
