Design Rationale: Pure Ward Bed Decisions

1. Strict Non-Duplication:

On this ward page, other tabs already own operational details: Beds owns the physical floor map and room hygiene; Arrivals owns transport ETAs and patient handover notes; Discharges owns discharge checklists, pharmacy TTAs, and medical clearance. The Decisions tab strictly owns human clinical & operational sign-offs that change systemic state.

2. What constitutes a "Ward Decision"?

  • Shift Capacity Handshake (Gate 1): NUM declaring staffed allocatable beds vs physical beds with active acuity limiters.
  • Inbound Intake Authority (Gate 2): Accepting to a specific bed, clinically declining with mandatory governance reason, or deferring for senior MO review.
  • Discharge Release Gate & Barrier Escalation (Gate 3): Authorizing departure to release physical bed to Environmental Services; escalating systemic barriers (such as NDIS housing lease holds).
  • Legal & Safety Governance (Gate 4): Authorizing Mental Health Act Section 17 leave returns/extensions/breaches; placing or lifting bed infection restrictions.

3. Why Option 1 + Option 2 Synthesis Wins:

Option 1 provided instant action-ranked urgency for immediate triage at the nursing station, but lacked shift timeline context. Option 2 grounded the day in chronological shift gates (Gate 1 through 4) and introduced barrier escalation (NDIS delays). Unified Architecture 1 merges both: An executive milestone progress ribbon anchors the shift rhythm at a glance, while action-ranked cards and barrier management ensure zero clinical decisions fall through the cracks.
