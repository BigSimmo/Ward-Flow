# Pilot competency check

For: trainers, access admins and staff joining the shadow pilot. Status: current guidance.
Owner: pilot lead (to be named).

_Updated 2026-11-08 - written for the shadow pilot, tasks matched to the
[quick-start sheets](quick-start/README.md); Documentation owns._

A short check before an account goes live. The person does three tasks for their desk in demo
mode, with a trainer watching. The access admin gives the role only after a pass is signed off
([account process](account-process.md)).

## How to run it

1. Open the sign-in page, press **Explore the demo** and enter the demo code. The demo runs on
   made-up records only.
2. Open **Settings**, then **Demo tools**. Under **See it as**, choose the person's desk.
3. Give the person their [quick-start sheet](quick-start/README.md). They may read it during the
   check.
4. Ask for the three tasks below, one at a time. Do not do them for the person.
5. About 15 minutes per person.

Never use real patient details in the check, the notes or the sign-off table.

## How the trainer marks it

Each task is **Pass** or **Not yet**. A task passes when the person:

- uses the right screen and button without being shown,
- for a task that records something, sees the short message saying it was recorded, and can say
  where to look if it was not,
- for a task that only reads, saves or shows (the executive and TV board tasks), finds the right
  figure, file or screen and says what it shows,
- can say one thing the app will not do for that task (for example, it never suggests a ward).

All three tasks must pass. A prompt about where a button is counts as **Not yet**.

## Tasks by desk

| Desk                                             | Task 1                                                                           | Task 2                                                                                              | Task 3                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bed coordinator                                  | **Decide** a referral: **Accept referral** or **Decline referral** with a reason | **Pull the bed** for an accepted referral                                                           | **Book transport**, or record that none is needed                           |
| Ward (nurse in charge)                           | **Confirm your beds** and press **Confirm**                                      | Open a referral, then **Accept in principle**, or **Decline** with a reason and **Confirm decline** | **Confirm arrival** for an incoming patient                                 |
| ED mental health clinician                       | Send a **New referral**                                                          | **Record medically cleared** and **Mark handover ready** on a patient                               | **Mark Form 1A received**, typing the expiry time from a made-up paper form |
| Site bed manager                                 | Check ward bed figures and say who confirmed one                                 | Record what is holding up a discharge on **Discharges**                                             | **Open on TV** from **Capacity**, then leave the TV view                    |
| Transport officer                                | Open a job and use **Inspect form**                                              | Move a job on with **Mark accepted** and **Mark en route**                                          | Press a step, then **Undo** it from the message                             |
| Community team                                   | **Send referral** with **Community follow-up** or **Assess for admission**       | Find it on the **Sent** tab and say whether a ward accepted                                         | **Record contact** for a referral to the team                               |
| Executive (read only)                            | Find bed occupancy on **Statistics**                                             | Open **Capacity** and read one site's bed count                                                     | Save a table with **CSV**                                                   |
| Access admin                                     | Answer a request with **Give for 90 days** or **Decline**                        | **Give a role**: person, role, place and end date                                                   | **Remove** a role and confirm with **Remove role**                          |
| TV board (coordinator, site bed manager or ward) | **Open on TV** from **Capacity**                                                 | Use **Change screen** and **Full screen**                                                           | **Lock as TV**, then **Unlock** and **Leave TV view**                       |

The TV board is checked as part of the desk that opens it, not as a separate account.

## If not passed

- No account is given yet. Nothing is held against the person.
- The trainer shows the task once, then the person tries again later the same day or at the next
  session.
- After a second **Not yet**, the trainer tells the pilot lead, who arranges more time with the
  desk guide.
- Write the result in the sign-off table either way.
