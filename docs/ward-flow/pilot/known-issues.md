# Known issues for pilot staff

<!-- last-checked: 2026-11-08 -->

_Created 2026-11-08 - staff-facing known issues, built from repository sources at `main` `d85618e6`; Documentation owns._

**For:** staff using Ward Flow in the shadow pilot. **Status:** current. **Last checked:**
8 November 2026, against the repository only. Nobody checked the hosted site for this page.

Each item says what you may notice, what to do, and where it comes from. Nothing here is marked
fixed or broken unless the source says so. If something is not on this list, log it in the
[incident log](incident-log.md).

## What you may notice

| What you may notice                                                                                                                         | What to do                                                                                                                                               | Source                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Every patient is invented.** Names, beds and figures are made up.                                                                         | Never type a real patient's details. Use the whiteboard for every real decision. If real details are typed in, follow the [breach plan](breach-plan.md). | [Common questions](../desk-guides/common-questions.md#are-these-real-patients)                                                                              |
| **The first load can be slow** after nobody has used the signed-in site for a while. The site sleeps when idle.                             | Open Ward Flow a few minutes before the bed meeting. Wait for it to load. How long the wait is has not been measured.                                    | [Operations runbook](../OPERATIONS-RUNBOOK.md#common-incidents-first-steps) ("a slow first load is normal"); [hosting.md](../../hosting.md)                 |
| **"Connection unavailable" or a Retry message.**                                                                                            | Stay on the page and press **Retry**. Do not reload: a change that has not been sent is lost on reload. If it keeps happening, tell the bed coordinator. | [Common questions](../desk-guides/common-questions.md#what-if-the-connection-is-unavailable); [what's new](../STAFF-RELEASE-NOTES.md#21-to-24-october-2026) |
| **Shared saving on the hosted site is not fully tested.** Signed-in save, reload and conflict have not been checked in a browser there yet. | If two screens disagree, or a change goes missing, the whiteboard wins. Log it as a lost update.                                                         | [Runbook known gaps](../OPERATIONS-RUNBOOK.md#known-gaps); [live audit handoff](../live-audit-handoff-2026-11-06.md) (VERIFY-02)                            |
| **The TV board records nothing**, and may be switched off in some builds.                                                                   | Record decisions on your own desk. If the TV looks out of date, check a working desk; press **Retry** there if it shows.                                 | [TV board guide](../desk-guides/tv-board.md)                                                                                                                |
| **A screen will not open for you.** Each role opens only its own screens.                                                                   | Check the table in "Why can't I open this?". Ask the access admin if you need a screen.                                                                  | [Why can't I open this?](../desk-guides/why-cant-i.md)                                                                                                      |
| **A change in "What's new" is not on the hosted site yet.** Source changes reach the site only at a release.                                | Check with the bed coordinator. During a pilot, releases are agreed to go out by hand once a week, with sites told the day before.                       | [What's new](../STAFF-RELEASE-NOTES.md); [runbook, during a pilot](../OPERATIONS-RUNBOOK.md#during-a-pilot)                                                 |
| **Typed text comes back as "Typed text not kept on this device"** after a reload in the demonstration.                                      | This is on purpose. Retype it if you still need it.                                                                                                      | [What's new](../STAFF-RELEASE-NOTES.md#21-to-24-october-2026)                                                                                               |
| **Ward Flow never picks the bed** or ranks wards, and flags never move a patient up the list.                                               | A person always chooses. This is by design, not a fault.                                                                                                 | [Common questions](../desk-guides/common-questions.md#does-the-app-choose-the-bed)                                                                          |
| **No Mental Health Act times are worked out.** Legal forms use the due times someone typed.                                                 | Check the form itself and your usual process for Act times.                                                                                              | [TV board guide](../desk-guides/tv-board.md); [STATUS, what is built](../STATUS.md#what-is-built)                                                           |
| **Transport is not booked in the app.** Log booking stays greyed until a ward accepts.                                                      | Book by phone first, then log it with the CAD number, ETA, provider, legal status and escort.                                                            | [Common questions](../desk-guides/common-questions.md#why-cant-i-book-transport)                                                                            |
| **No link to the hospital record system.** Nothing comes in from or goes out to it.                                                         | Keep using your usual systems. Ward Flow is a side-by-side trial only.                                                                                   | [AGENTS.md, prototype operating mode](../../../AGENTS.md#prototype-operating-mode-approved-3-october-2026)                                                  |
| **iPhone and iPad Safari** have not been checked on a real device.                                                                          | If something looks wrong on an Apple device, log it with the device type.                                                                                | [Live audit handoff](../live-audit-handoff-2026-11-06.md) (ENV-02)                                                                                          |

## Keeping this page current

- Recheck it **weekly during the pilot** and after each release. Update the date in the
  `last-checked` marker and the line at the top only after checking each source again.
- Add an item when the [incident log](incident-log.md) shows staff tripping over the same thing
  twice. Remove one only when its source says it is fixed and released.
- Every item needs a source file. Do not add guesses.
- When a visible step changes, update the matching [desk guide](../desk-guides/README.md) and
  [what's new](../STAFF-RELEASE-NOTES.md) in the same change.
