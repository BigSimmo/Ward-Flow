# Quick start: Access admin

For: staff using this desk in the shadow pilot. Status: current. Owner: pilot lead (to be named).

_Updated 2026-11-08 - written for the shadow pilot from the desk guide and source at `d85618e6`; Documentation owns._

## What you use Ward Flow for

You give staff access to the right desk and place, and take it away. You never see
patients. Your work is in **Settings**, then **Access**. Follow the
[pilot account process](../account-process.md).

## Signing in

1. Open the pilot web address (to be named by the pilot lead).
2. Type your account and password, then press **Sign in**. Use **Forgot password?** if you need a
   new one. Never share a password.
3. You land on **Settings**. If you hold more than one desk, use **Switch desk** in the account menu.
4. When you finish, press **Sign out**. The app also locks itself after a short time without use.

New here? Open **Help** and choose **Take the tour**.

## Most common tasks

1. **Answer a request.** Under **Requests for access**, press **Give for 90 days** or
   **Decline**. Give only when the request form and competency sign-off are on file.
2. **Give a role.** Under **Give a role**, choose **Person**, **Role**, **Place** and **Ends**,
   then press **Give role**.
3. **Remove access.** Under **Everyone with a role**, press **Remove**, then **Remove role**. Do it
   the same day someone leaves or changes job.
4. **Review.** Each month, check **Everyone with a role**, **Ending soon** roles and the **Access
   log**.

Nobody gives themselves a role. Cover for a shift is given by the coordinator or the ward or ED,
not by you.

## Do not use it for

- **Not a clinical record.** Keep writing in the real notes and on the ward whiteboard.
- **No clinical decisions.** Ward Flow never ranks patients, suggests a ward or works out Mental
  Health Act times. People decide.
- **Synthetic data only.** In the shadow pilot every patient is invented. Never type a real name,
  UMRN or detail, in the app, a screenshot or a message about it.

## Who to contact

- Pilot lead: to be named.
- Access admin (accounts, roles, removing access): to be named.
- Something looks wrong or unsafe: tell the pilot lead the same shift, and keep working from the
  whiteboard and phone.
