## Design adaptations

- The static drawing's compact schematic was adapted to the live provider's full unit set rather than reducing or fabricating nodes.
- The drawing's capacity squares remain paired with visible text and counts so color is never the only meaning.
- Recorded destinations remain visually and semantically separate from eligible and ineligible candidate routes.
- Long operational explanations are available on demand; refusal reasons, gate results and candidate verdicts remain visible where decisions are made.
- The shortlist does not synthesize a name, demographic fact, contact, bed state, destination or recommendation.
