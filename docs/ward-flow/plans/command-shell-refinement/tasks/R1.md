## Design adaptation to verify visually

The desktop workspace now deliberately uses a `64rem` minimum region-grid height and document
scrolling at `>= 1400px` (`coordinator.module.css:3422-3477`). This follows the latest owner direction
to keep lower panels usable instead of compressing them into the drawing's viewport-locked tracks,
so it is not reported as a mockup-conformance defect. It does mean the queue, diagram, register and
shortlist headers leave the viewport as the document moves, even though their content bodies retain
local scroll ownership. The 1440px acceptance pass should record whether “fixed headers” means
fixed within each panel body (the current interpretation) or viewport-pinned while traversing the
taller workspace. Do not shrink the lower register back to the earlier `11.5rem` compromise merely
to match the drawing.
