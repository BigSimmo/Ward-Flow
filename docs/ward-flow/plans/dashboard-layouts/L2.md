## Shared-variable dependency and limitations

- The route modules consume the root-owned `--ward-workspace-height`, `--ward-section-height`, `--ward-compact-height`, and `--ward-sticky-top` variables. Each use carries the plan's exact fallback value, so worker/root sequencing cannot temporarily collapse a layout.
- No test, typecheck, browser run, or visual claim was made: the assignment reserves those checks for root. The JSX changes are limited to semantic labels and keyboard focusability on the explicit scroll regions, including the existing statistics panel bodies that now scroll at desktop widths.
- Root browser inspection at 1440x1000 found CSS min-content propagation expanding the 856px referral register workspace to a 3218px queue and stretching a queue heading to 864px. The referral-only correction gives the parent a `minmax(0, 1fr)` row, permits the queue/detail columns to shrink with `min-height: 0`, and sizes each section's internal rows from content with `align-content: start`. Root retains the browser recheck.
- Every `.panelBody` that becomes a desktop vertical scroller was inventoried across Overview, Ward, Community, ED, and Service: 42 bodies now accept keyboard focus and carry a contextual group label inside their already named `WardPanel`. Compare bodies remain natural and were not made focusable.
- Visual confirmation is still required for 1001px, a wide desktop, a short desktop viewport, phone, and print. In particular, verify sticky referral headings over selected-detail content and the statistics service column allocations with the longest fixture content.
- Existing tables, diagrams, and the hub/search long lists retain their own internal scrolling where their content remains exceptional on a phone.
