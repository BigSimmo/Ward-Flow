# L5 — independent Q003 source review

Scope: one adversarial source pass over the Q003 delta against the fixed L1–L4 pre-edit snapshots. No browser, tests, provider calls or Git mutations were performed. The Handover nested-grid compression and Referrals parent-grid minimum-size failures found during integration were corrected by root/L2 while this pass was open, so they are not repeated below.

## Findings

### P1 — the 1001px desktop transition clips or horizontally overflows the Hub, Search and Referrals workspaces

At 1001px the shell remains a row and the open rail consumes `14.75rem` (`shell/ward-rail.module.css:17-27`; `ward-flow-layout.module.css:22-49`), leaving about 765px before page padding. Q003 nevertheless restores two-column layouts at 1001px with hard minimums of 28rem + 20rem on Hub (`hub/hub.module.css:825-849`) and Search (`search/search.module.css:1091-1099`), and 30rem + 24rem on Referrals (`referrals/referrals.module.css:2601-2606`). The Search main also clips x overflow (`search/search.module.css:74-82`). In the open-rail state these grids require more width than exists even before their gaps and 48px page padding, so the right panel is clipped or the page gains horizontal overflow throughout a material desktop interval. This blocks reliable use of the detail/access pane. Keep the single-column fallback until the post-rail content width can hold the fixed minimums, or use shrinkable `minmax(0, …)` tracks. Smallest proof: inspect all three routes at 1001px with the rail open and assert `scrollWidth === clientWidth` plus full right-panel visibility.

### P2 — newly bounded Community, Statistics and Capacity content is not exposed as a labelled keyboard scroll region

Q003 makes every Community `.panelBody` an internal `overflow-y: auto` owner (`community/community.module.css:1013-1036`), but the existing bodies remain plain unfocusable divs (`community/community-screen.tsx:532`, repeated through line 1003). Statistics does the same centrally (`statistics/statistics-sections.module.css:624-638`) across plain bodies such as `statistics/statistics-ward-screen.tsx:242`, while Capacity scrolls the entire map and summary `WardPanel`s (`capacity/capacity.module.css:1030-1061`) without adding a focusable labelled body at all (`capacity/capacity-screen.tsx:263-339`). Keyboard-only users therefore have no explicit target from which to scroll offscreen rows or figures, contrary to Q003's labelled keyboard-scroll-region contract. Add a named `role="region"`/`aria-label`/`tabIndex={0}` to each actual body owner; Capacity needs body wrappers so the panel header stays outside the scroller. Smallest proof: tab through a deliberately overflowing Community, Statistics and Capacity panel and scroll to its last row without using a pointer.

### P2 — the Statistics landing page does not align the Community/Referrals pair

`landingRegions` stretches its direct grid items, but the Community item is a wrapper and its child panel receives only `max-height`, not `height: 100%` (`statistics/statistics-landing-third-edition.module.css:63-78`, `342-363`; wrapper markup at `statistics/statistics-screen.tsx:965-993`). Its paired Referrals panel is a direct grid item and stretches to the row height. When Referrals is taller, the Community panel keeps its intrinsic height inside the stretched wrapper, leaving the neighbouring bottom edges visibly misaligned despite the Q003 requirement. Make the wrapped Community panel fill the wrapper (or remove the wrapper as a sizing boundary). Smallest proof: compare the two panel bottom coordinates at wide desktop with the longest fixture content.

## Residual verification boundary

Root integration resolved the three findings: the narrow desktop grids now stack at their appropriate breakpoints (1001px captures inspected); Capacity/Community/Statistics have explicit keyboard targets; the landing Community panel fills its wrapper, with Community and Referrals measured at identical 1711px tops and 2543px bottoms.
