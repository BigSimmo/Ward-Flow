# M1 — Movements page refinement

## Outcome

Implemented the Movements-only refinement against the existing engine data and the third-edition
drawing structure.

- Bounded the traffic schematic inside a 32rem scrolling viewport and reduced its working canvas
  from 65rem to 51.25rem. Emergency-department and ward nodes now sit on a compact, evenly spaced
  grid with distinct orthogonal lanes, endpoint accepted/declined totals, and rounded elbows.
- Added a corridor rail switcher for **Accepted** and **Declined**. Accepted means the existing open
  movement has an accepting ward. Declined is derived only from actual same-day `Decline` records,
  including their controlled reasons; it does not borrow a later journey stage.
- Omitted the drawing's **Unused** category because the current records provide no truthful unused
  corridor fact or count. The expandable key states this limit.
- Kept Day figures and the tier-1 attention actions, with shorter operational labels.
- Made the movement worklist independently scroll within a 44rem desktop body while its order
  controls remain visible. The right summary stays fixed in the desktop composition.
- Replaced the duplicate full transport list with the retained **Transport right now** rail and a
  five-way summary switcher: Stage, Transport, Waiting, Resolved, and No owner. Every count and row
  comes from the existing movement, closure, owner, stage, and transport derivations.
- Preserved the movement detail drawer and every `Review patient` route.
- Kept the compact synthetic-record disclosure and reconciliation wording.

## Deviations from the drawing

- The drawing's carried/completed traffic meaning was not used because `corridorCounts` describes
  current open accepted movements. The UI says **Accepted** and **active corridors** instead.
- The drawing's illustrative unused-corridor tab was omitted because it cannot be calculated from
  the current records without inventing a fact.
- The right-hand summaries use current open-stage counts and actual same-day closures; illustrative
  mockup numbers were not copied.

## Addendum — transport fact preservation

Source review found that removing the separate full transport panel also removed its per-leg facts:
provider, origin, destination, state, elapsed time since booking, and the patient route. Restored the
existing `TransportRow` fact layer inside the bounded **Transport** view of **Shape of the open
day**. The compact header still states transport-leg and no-leg counts, while selecting Transport
reveals one fact-complete row per current leg without recreating a second page-length panel.

Also shortened stage empty-state and deadline copy while retaining the actual sorting behavior.
The two assigned DOM contracts now select the Transport view before asserting per-leg duplication,
facts, state words, elapsed clocks, and patient routes. They also characterize the six-panel page,
the Movement worklist title, the open-stage summary, and the Accepted/Declined corridor meanings.
