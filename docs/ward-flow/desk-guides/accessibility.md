# Desk guide: Seeing and using Ward Flow

<!-- last-checked: 2026-10-27 -->

This guide is for every desk. It covers the colour code, the display settings and the keyboard.

## The colour code works without colour

Each colour has its own shape, everywhere in the app, and the word beside it says what it means.

- **Triangle**: act now.
- **Filled amber circle**: at risk.
- **Tick**: done.
- **Capsule**: moving.
- **Ring**: waiting.
- **Cross**: declined or closed.

A screen reader reads the word, not the shape.

## Display settings

Open **Settings**, then **Display**.

- **Theme**: Auto, Light, Dark or Glare. Glare is a bright, plain light screen for strong light. It stays light even if your device is dark.
- **High contrast**: stronger edges and ink. Faint lines and grey words become full ink.
- **Reduce motion**: stops slides and pulses.

## Keyboard

Help lists the keyboard shortcuts. **Ctrl+K** (Cmd+K on a Mac) always opens search. Settings can turn off the single-key shortcuts. Shortcuts never fire while you are typing in a field.
