# Desk guide: ED mental health clinician

<!-- last-checked: 2026-11-11 -->

You look after mental health patients waiting in your own emergency department.

## What you see first

**My ED board**: your department's patients, longest wait first, and where each referral stands.
Your main menu: **My ED board, My referrals, Find a bed**.

On a phone, the bar at the bottom has **My ED, Referrals, Create, Alerts, Find a bed**. **Create**
holds **New referral**.

## Jobs you do most

1. **New referral.** Press **New** in the header and choose **Referral** (**Triage** is the other
   choice). Fill in the form and send it.
2. **Record ED steps.** On a patient, use **Record examination**, **Record medically cleared** and
   **Mark handover ready**.
3. **Legal paperwork.** Use **Change legal status** and **Mark Form 1A received**. Type any expiry
   time from the paper form. The app never works it out.
4. **Transport.** Once a bed is pulled for your patient, press **Transport booked**, or **No
   transport needed**. The board shows it as "Pulled".
5. **Fix a mistake.** Use **Withdraw referral**, **Record urgency change** or **Correct receipt**.

## What you cannot do

- The board never re-sorts the queue for you. It stays longest wait first.
- You cannot see other wards' reasons for declining.
- You cannot accept for a ward or place a patient.
- The bed meeting TV view is not part of the ED desk.

## Saved, or not

- After an action, a short message says it was recorded. In the demo it adds "Saved on this
  device only."
- A success message now waits until the save is confirmed. Until then, what you typed stays in the
  form.
- On a shared board a small "Saving…" note shows while a save goes through. If it takes longer
  than 8 seconds, or you are offline, the status line warns you not to reload.
- If it reads "Connection unavailable — changes are disabled." and shows **Retry**: stay on the
  page and press **Retry**. Do not reload. Reloading loses the unconfirmed action.
- A message saying an action was not recorded means nothing changed. Read the reason and try again.
- If Retry keeps failing, phone the coordinator and keep paper notes.

New here? Open **Help** and choose **Take the tour**.
