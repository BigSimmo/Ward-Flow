# Desk guide: Bed meeting TV board

<!-- last-checked: 2026-10-24 -->

The TV view puts the bed meeting on a wall screen. It is for looking at together, not for
recording. It may be switched off in some builds.

## Who opens it

The flow coordinator, a site bed manager or a ward. Open **Capacity** and press **Open on TV**.
ED desks do not open the TV view.

## What it shows

- **Today at a glance**: beds ready now, who is going out and who is coming in.
- **Waiting in ED** and the **Longest ED wait**, in wait order.
- **Due out today and tomorrow**, and people past their expected date.
- **Delays by cause**.
- **Agreed at the bed meeting**: the actions, their owner and when they are due.

## Running the meeting

1. Open the TV view on the shared screen before the meeting starts.
2. Work down the screen from top to bottom.
3. Record any decision on your own desk, not on the TV.
4. When an agreed action is finished, the owner marks it **Done** on their own desk.
5. Press **Leave TV view** when the meeting ends.

## What it cannot do

- It records nothing. Every change is made on a desk, and the TV picks it up.
- It never suggests a ward, scores a patient or re-orders the waiting list.
- It shows no Mental Health Act times it worked out itself.

## Saved, or not

The TV saves nothing. If the screen looks out of date, check the status line on a working desk. If
that desk shows **Retry**, press it there. Do not rely on the TV until the desk is saving again.

_Prototype on synthetic data. Not for real patients._
