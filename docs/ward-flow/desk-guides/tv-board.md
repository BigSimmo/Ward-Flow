# Desk guide: Bed meeting TV board

<!-- last-checked: 2026-11-10 -->

The TV view puts the bed meeting on a wall screen. It is for looking at together, not for
recording. It may be switched off in some builds.

## Who opens it

The bed coordinator or a site bed manager: open **Capacity** and press **Open on TV**. A ward:
press **On TV** on **My ward** for that ward's own screen. ED desks do not open the TV view.

## What it shows

- Bed figures **By health service**, or for one service the **Wards in** that service.
- **Beds that fit**, and for all services the **Brief for this meeting**.
- **Longest ED waits**, in wait order.
- **Waiting by cause**.
- A ward's own screen shows **Today at a glance**: beds ready now, who is coming in, who is
  **Going out** today and tomorrow, and people **Past expected date**.

## Running the meeting

1. Open the TV view on the shared screen before the meeting starts. Use **Change screen** to pick
   another TV view and **Full screen** to fill the wall screen.
2. On a TV that stays on the wall, press **Lock as TV**. The browser then shows only TV views, even
   after a reload, and **Leave TV view** becomes **Unlock**. Unlocking asks for the account's
   password in the shared workspace.
3. Work down the screen from top to bottom.
4. Record any decision on your own desk, not on the TV.
5. When an agreed action is finished, the owner marks it **Done** on their own desk.
6. Press **Leave TV view** (or **Unlock**, then **Leave TV view**) when the meeting ends.

## What it cannot do

- It records nothing. Every change is made on a desk, and the TV picks it up.
- It never suggests a ward, scores a patient or re-orders the waiting list.
- It shows no Mental Health Act times it worked out itself.

## Saved, or not

The TV saves nothing. If the screen looks out of date, check the status line on a working desk. If
that desk shows **Retry**, press it there. Do not rely on the TV until the desk is saving again.

_Prototype on synthetic data. Not for real patients._
