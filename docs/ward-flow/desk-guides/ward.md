# Desk guide: Ward (nurse in charge)

<!-- last-checked: 2026-10-26 -->

_Updated 2026-10-30 — role wording checked against source at `b6aa5cde`; browser coverage above is unchanged._

You run your own ward's beds and answer the referrals sent to it. This desk is **Nurse in charge**,
and new records name it **Nurse in charge** (31 Oct 2026). Records saved before then say **Ward
manager** and are kept as they are.

## What you see first

**My ward**: your bed board, what needs doing this shift, and **Awaiting your answer** for
referrals. Your main menu: **My ward, Coming and going, Referrals to answer, Handover**.

On a phone, the bar at the bottom has **My ward, Referrals, Create, Alerts, Handover**. **Coming
and going** is under **Menu**.

## Jobs you do most

1. **Confirm your beds.** Each morning, fill in **Confirm your beds** and press **Confirm** by the
   roll-up time. The time is your service's default, not a legal limit.
2. **Read the referral.** Tap the patient's name on the incoming card, or **View referral**. You
   see the full referral, with name, UMRN and attached documents, before you answer.
3. **Answer it.** Choose the pull time limit (standard or extra travel), then press **Accept in
   principle**. It saves in one tap, and the message that appears has **Undo** if you pressed it by
   mistake. Or press **Decline**, pick a reason and **Confirm decline**.
4. **Arrivals.** When the patient reaches the ward, press **Confirm Arrival**.
5. **Away and discharge.** On a bed, use **Mark at an ED** when your patient goes to an ED, and
   **Mark them back** when they return. Record a discharge with **They have left**.
6. **Hand over.** Open **Handover**, check the sheet, then **Sign off**.

The card shows the patient's checks on one line and your ward's own state on another, so a stale
bed count is not read as a problem with the patient.

## What you cannot do

- You never see which other wards or teams the patient was referred to.
- You cannot change another ward's beds or answers.
- The app does not work out Mental Health Act times. It shows only times someone typed.
- A patient's own bed is "held" only while they are on leave or absent. A bed kept for an incoming
  patient is "Pulled".

## Saved, or not

- After an action, a short message says it was recorded. In the demo it adds "Saved on this
  device only."
- On a shared board the status line reads "Saving shared changes…", then your desk name again.
- If it reads "Connection unavailable — changes are disabled." and shows **Retry**: stay on the
  page and press **Retry**. Do not reload. Reloading loses the unconfirmed action.
- A message saying an action was not recorded means nothing changed. Read the reason and try again.
- If Retry keeps failing, use the ward whiteboard, and phone the coordinator.
