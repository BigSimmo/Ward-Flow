# Desk guide: Transport officer

<!-- last-checked: 2026-10-24 -->

You move patients between places for your own health service.

## What you see first

**My jobs**: the open transport jobs for your service, with the flags recorded on each referral.
Your main menu: **My jobs, Alerts**.

## Jobs you do most

1. **Open a job.** Choose a job from the list to see the booking, the forms and the arrival plan.
2. **Move it along.** Press **Mark accepted**, then **Mark en route**, **Mark collected** and
   **Mark delivered** as each step happens.
3. **Undo a slip.** If you pressed the wrong step, use **Undo** on the message straight away.
4. **Divert.** If the patient must go somewhere else, use **Divert** and record the diversion.
5. **Check the forms.** Use **Inspect form** to check the forms pack before you leave.
6. **Hand over.** Use **Copy handover** for a plain summary of open jobs.

You get phone alerts when a job is new or changes.

## What you cannot do

- You see only your own service's jobs.
- You cannot accept a referral, pull a bed or change a ward's beds.
- A step can be refused, for example if a legal order changed while the bed was still pulled. The
  message says why. The coordinator sorts it out first.

## Saved, or not

- After each step, a short message says it was recorded, or why it was refused. In the demo it adds
  "Saved on this device only."
- On a shared board the status line reads "Saving shared changes…", then your desk name again.
- If it reads "Connection unavailable — changes are disabled." and shows **Retry**: stay on the
  page and press **Retry**. Do not reload. Reloading loses the unconfirmed action.
- A message saying an action was not recorded means nothing changed. Read the reason and try again.
- If Retry keeps failing, phone the coordinator and the receiving ward.

New here? Open **Help** and choose **Take the tour**.
