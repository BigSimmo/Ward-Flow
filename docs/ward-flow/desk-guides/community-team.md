# Desk guide: Community clinician

<!-- last-checked: 2026-10-26 -->

You work for one community mental health team. You send referrals and answer the ones sent to
your team.

## What you see first

**My team**: referrals waiting for your team's answer, people in a bed, and your caseload. Your
main menu: **My team, Referrals, New referral**. **Movements**, under **More**, shows your own
journeys.

On a phone, the bar at the bottom has **My team, Referrals, Create, Alerts, Activity**. **Create**
holds **New referral**, and **Movements** is under **Menu**.

## Jobs you do most

1. **Send a referral.** Use **New referral** or **Raise referral**. For a referral to a community
   team, choose the purpose (**Community follow-up** or **Assess for admission**) and the first
   contact time, then **Send referral**.
2. **Check what you sent.** The **Sent** tab shows your referrals and whether a ward accepted.
3. **Answer a referral to your team.** Open it, then **Accept referral** or **Decline referral**.
4. **Record contact.** Use **Record contact** after you reach the person.
5. **Follow a move.** An accepted referral can be moved, transported and arrived. Follow it on
   **Movements**.
6. **Fix your own referral.** Correct it, mark information **Provided**, or change urgency or legal
   status. You can do this only for referrals your team sent.

## What you cannot do

- You cannot accept for a ward or place a patient.
- You cannot see other teams' referrals.
- No addresses are recorded for community journeys. The journey shows your team name only.

## Saved, or not

- After an action, a short message says it was recorded. In the demo it adds "Saved on this
  device only."
- A contact is shown as saved only once the save is confirmed.
- On a shared board the status line reads "Saving shared changes…", then your desk name again.
- If it reads "Connection unavailable — changes are disabled." and shows **Retry**: stay on the
  page and press **Retry**. Do not reload. Reloading loses the unconfirmed action.
- A message saying an action was not recorded means nothing changed. Read the reason and try again.
- If Retry keeps failing, phone the coordinator and keep paper notes.

You also get phone alerts when a referral is answered, or when a client in your catchment is
admitted or discharged.

New here? Open **Help** and choose **Take the tour**.
