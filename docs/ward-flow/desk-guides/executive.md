# Desk guide: Executive (read only)

<!-- last-checked: 2026-10-26 -->

_Updated 2026-10-30 — role wording checked against source at `b6aa5cde`; browser coverage above is unchanged._

You see how the network or your health service is going, as numbers. You do not see patients.

## What you see first

**Statistics** is your home. Your menu also opens **Capacity** for bed counts across your network or
health service. Both screens are read only. On a phone, your own **Settings** is under **Menu**.

## Jobs you do most

1. **See the network.** **Statistics** shows beds, occupancy and ED waits in one place.
2. **Check beds.** Open **Capacity** for bed counts across your network or health service.
3. **Compare.** Use the ward and ED comparisons to set two wards or departments side by side.
4. **Look for pressure.** Check ED waits, blocked discharges and expected discharges.
5. **Export.** Use **CSV** to save a table of figures.

## How the numbers work

- Counts of people under a small number read "fewer than 5", so a person cannot be picked out.
- Bed counts and bed states (in use, pulled, ready) stay exact.
- Lists of people are not shown.
- A figure nobody recorded says "Not recorded". The app does not guess.

## What you cannot do

- You cannot record, change or approve anything. Your desk has no actions.
- You cannot open patient records, referrals, wards or the bed board.
- You do not send or receive broadcast alerts.

## Saved, or not

You do not save anything, so there is nothing to confirm.

- On a shared board the status line shows your desk name.
- If it reads "Connection unavailable — changes are disabled." and shows **Retry**: press
  **Retry** to get the latest figures. Until then the numbers may be out of date.

_Prototype on synthetic data. Not for real patients._
