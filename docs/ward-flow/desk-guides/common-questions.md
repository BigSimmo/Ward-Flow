# Desk guide: Common questions

<!-- last-checked: 2026-10-28 -->

Short answers to questions staff ask often. Each answer is copied from Help in the app, and the line under it says where.

## Saving and connection

### How do I know an action was saved?

> After an action, a short message says it was recorded.

Source: Help, Saved or not (`SAVED_OR_NOT`).

### What if it says an action was not recorded?

> A message saying an action was not recorded means nothing changed. Read the reason and try again.

Source: Help, Saved or not (`SAVED_OR_NOT`).

### What if the connection is unavailable?

> If the app says the connection is unavailable, stay on the page and press Retry. Do not reload.

Source: Help, Saved or not (`SAVED_OR_NOT`).

### What if Ward Flow is down?

> Tell the bed coordinator on shift. They decide when everyone switches to paper and pass the problem to the Ward Flow team.

Source: Help, When it is down (`WHEN_IT_IS_DOWN`). Screen: Downtime.

## Referrals and beds

### Why can't I answer this referral?

> Only the ward itself accepts or declines its referrals. A bed manager sees them but cannot answer, so ask the ward to answer from its own desk.

Source: Help, screen questions (`screenHelpFor`). Screen: Referrals.

### How do I ask more wards?

> In the Bed box choose Ask wards, or Ask more wards once some have been asked, then choose the wards. Wards are never ranked, and a ward that declines frees its place.

Source: Help, screen questions (`screenHelpFor`). Screen: Movements.

### How do I pull a bed?

> Once a ward accepts, the Bed box shows Pull the bed. Choose it and pick the bed. The bed is kept for the patient until the pull time limit.

Source: Help, screen questions (`screenHelpFor`). Screen: Movements.

### How do I change a destination?

> Open Destination on Now to ask other wards or move a pulled bed. If the plan has changed completely, Withdraw referral closes the movement and frees any pulled bed.

Source: Help, screen questions (`screenHelpFor`). Screen: Movements.

### Does the app choose the bed?

> A person always chooses the bed. Nothing is placed automatically.

Source: Help, Bed coordinator desk guide limits (`DESK_GUIDES.coordinator`).

### Do flags move a patient up the list?

> Wait lists stay in wait order. Flags highlight a row but never move it.

Source: Help, Bed coordinator desk guide limits (`DESK_GUIDES.coordinator`).

### Can a ward see where else a patient was referred?

> You never see which other wards the patient was referred to.

Source: Help, Nurse in charge desk guide limits (`DESK_GUIDES.ward`).

## Transport

### Why can't I book transport?

> Log booking stays greyed until a ward has accepted the patient, and the reason shows beside it. Book by phone first: logging it needs the CAD number from the call, the quoted ETA, the provider, the legal status and whether an escort is needed.

Source: Help, screen questions (`screenHelpFor`). Screen: Movements.

### Why was a transport step refused?

> A step can be refused, for example when a legal order changed. The message says why.

Source: Help, Transport officer desk guide limits (`DESK_GUIDES.officer`).

## Meetings and figures

### How do I hand over to the next shift?

> Read Act now first, then what is due before the next handover. Work through Before you sign, then choose Sign off. Use More to print the sheet or present it at the meeting.

Source: Help, screen questions (`screenHelpFor`). Screen: Handover.

### Why does a figure say "fewer than 5"?

> Small counts of people read "fewer than 5", so no one can be picked out.

Source: Help, Executive desk guide limits (`DESK_GUIDES.executive`).

### Why does a figure say Not recorded?

> A figure nobody recorded says Not recorded. The app does not guess.

Source: Help, Executive desk guide limits (`DESK_GUIDES.executive`).

## About the data

### Are these real patients?

> Invented. Every patient, bed figure and record in Ward Flow is made up.

Source: Help, Glossary, Synthetic (`GLOSSARY`).
