# Desk guide: Access admin

<!-- last-checked: 2026-10-24 -->

You give staff access to the right desk and place. You never see patients.

## What you see first

**Settings**, where access is managed.

## Jobs you do most

1. **Answer a request.** When someone asks for access, press **Give for 90 days** or **Decline**.
2. **Give a role.** Choose the person, one desk (for example Nurse in charge) and one place (for
   example their ward), then press **Give role**. Every grant has an end date within a set ceiling.
3. **Cover.** Use **Give cover** only for someone who already holds a ward or ED role. Press
   **End cover** when it is no longer needed.
4. **Remove access.** Press **Remove** when a person changes job or leaves.
5. **Check the list.** Review who holds which desk and place, and when each grant ends.

## What you cannot do

- You cannot open patient records, referrals or bed boards.
- You cannot break glass on a record.
- You cannot record any clinical or bed flow action.

## Saved, or not

- After a change, a short message says it was recorded.
- On a shared board the status line reads "Saving shared changes…", then your desk name again.
- If it reads "Connection unavailable — changes are disabled." and shows **Retry**: stay on the
  page and press **Retry**. Do not reload. Reloading loses the unconfirmed change.
- A message saying a change was not recorded means nothing changed. Read the reason and try again.

Grants work straight away in this prototype. Extra controls on role grants before any real data are still to be decided.

_Prototype on synthetic data. Not for real patients._
