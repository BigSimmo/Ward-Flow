# Desk guide: Site bed manager

<!-- last-checked: 2026-10-24 -->

You look after bed flow for one hospital site. You see your own site in detail and other sites as
bed counts only.

## What you see first

**Capacity**, for your site. Your main menu: **Capacity, Wards, Movements, Waiting, Handover**.

## Jobs you do most

1. **Check beds.** Check each ward's bed figures. The page shows who confirmed each figure. If
   one looks out of date, ask the coordinator to use **Request ward update**; only the
   coordinator can send it.
2. **Bed releases.** Flag, confirm or block a bed that is due to come free, from the ward page.
3. **Discharge blockers.** Record or clear what is holding up a discharge, on **Discharges**.
4. **Close or reopen a ward.** Choose a reason, then **Close [ward] to admissions**. Use
   **Reopen [ward] to admissions** when it can take patients again.
5. **Bed meeting.** Use **Open on TV** and **Meeting agenda**. Mark agreed actions **Done**.
6. **Alerts and notices.** Reply to a bed call and mark notices read.

## What you cannot do

- You cannot accept or decline a referral, place a patient or discharge a patient. These stay with
  the ward and the coordinator.
- **Referrals** is read only for you. It lists your own wards' incoming referrals.
- **Seclusion and restraint** shows what is in use and room status only. The ward records it.
- Handover sign-off stays with the ward and coordinator in this build.
- Other sites show as counts, with no patient names.

## Saved, or not

- After an action, a short message says it was recorded. In the demo it adds "Saved on this
  device only."
- On a shared board the status line reads "Saving shared changes…", then your desk name again.
- If it reads "Connection unavailable — changes are disabled." and shows **Retry**: stay on the
  page and press **Retry**. Do not reload. Reloading loses the unconfirmed action.
- A message saying an action was not recorded means nothing changed. Read the reason and try again.
- If Retry keeps failing, use the ward whiteboard and phone, and tell the coordinator.
