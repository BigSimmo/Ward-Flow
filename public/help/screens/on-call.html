On-call

BedChart · Who answers each on-call line now, for hospitals, teams and state lines.

Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

What is on the screen

  1. Who to call now. How many lines are answering, closed or due a check, then the state bed desk's number. Copy number copies it.
  2. Cover at. Shows who answers at a later time, such as 22:00 or 03:00. Choose Now to go back.
  3. Copy and Downtime card. Copy who is on copies the lines answering now. Downtime card holds the key lines and My list, with their hours, to print.
  4. Hospitals, Teams, State and Mine. Each tab is one part of the directory. Mine holds the lines you starred.
  5. Search. Search by role, hospital, ward, team or number.
  6. Show. Highlights lines that are answering, ending soon, have email or are due a check. Nothing is hidden.
  7. The directory. One line a role or place. Choose one to see who answers it, and star it to keep it under Mine.
  8. The chosen line. Who answers the line you chose, and until when, with every number and If no answer for who to try next.
  9. When it was checked. Each list says when someone last checked it. Mark checked now once you have confirmed it.

On a phone

  • More tools. Mine, Copy who is on and the Downtime card.

Jobs done here

  • Find who is on call. Open On-call and search by role, hospital, ward, team or number. A line opens on who answers now, with Copy number, and If no answer names who to try next. Use Cover at for a later time, and star a line to keep it under Mine.
  • Record that an on-call number was checked. Open On-call and choose the entry. Its card ends with when the number was last checked and who keeps it up to date. Once you have checked the number is right, press Mark checked now. It then reads Check recorded.

In BedChart, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.
