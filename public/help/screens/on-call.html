On-call

Ward Flow · Who answers each on-call line now, for hospitals, teams and state lines.

Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

What is on the screen

  1. Who to call now. The state lines, then bed flow for each service, then your starred lines. Copy a number straight from its card.
  2. The directory. Hospitals, Teams, State and Mine. Search by role, place or number, and star a line to keep it under Mine.
  3. The chosen line. Who answers the line you chose, and until when.
  4. When it was checked. Each list says when someone last checked it. Mark checked now once you have confirmed it.

On a phone

  • More tools. Cover at, to see who answers at a later time, and the other on-call tools.

Jobs done here

  • Find who is on call. Open On-call and search by role, hospital, ward, team or number. A line opens on who answers now, with Copy number, and If no answer names who to try next. Use Cover at for a later time, and star a line to keep it under Mine.
  • Record that an on-call number was checked. Open On-call and choose the entry. Once you have checked the number is right, press Mark checked now. The entry then shows when it was last checked, so others know how fresh it is.

In Ward Flow, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.
