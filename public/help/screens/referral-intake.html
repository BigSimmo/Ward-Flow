New referral

Ward Flow · Send a new referral to one or more wards, an ED or a community team.

Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

What is on the screen

  1. Refer to. A ward, a community team or an ED. The steps below change to suit.
  2. The patient. Find the patient, or choose Add patient for someone new. Not yet identified covers a person whose name is not known.
  3. Catchment. Worked out from the suburb on the record. Use this catchment to accept it, or choose another.
  4. Before you send. What is still needed, step by step. Send stays locked until it is all in.
  5. Save draft. Keeps the referral in this tab until it reloads, so you can come back to it.

On a phone

  • The referral so far. Where the referral is going. It reads Fill in and send until it has gone.
  • Continue. Takes you to the next step still needed. It sends once every step is in.

In Ward Flow, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.
