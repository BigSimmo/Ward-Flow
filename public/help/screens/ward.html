Ward

Ward Flow · One ward's beds, who is coming and going, and the decisions due this shift.

Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

What is on the screen

  1. This ward. Beds ready now, the bed list, and what the page is still checking.
  2. Place or move. Opens the bed board to place a patient in a bed or move one.
  3. Find on this ward. Find a bed or a person on this ward.
  4. The toolbar. On TV opens a read only screen for the ward's TV. More, beside it, holds Notices, Shift brief and Ward handover.
  5. Bed figures. Ready, pulled, closed and occupied beds, with when the ward last confirmed them.

On a phone

  • The round. Go through each patient in turn. The count shows how many are reviewed.

In Ward Flow, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.
