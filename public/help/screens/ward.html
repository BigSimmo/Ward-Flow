Ward

BedChart · One ward's beds, who is coming and going, and the decisions due this shift.

Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

What is on the screen

  1. This ward. Beds ready now, the bed list, and what the page is still checking.
  2. Ready and arrivals. Beds ready now and arrivals today. Open next opens the next decision due, or Open this shift goes to what needs doing.
  3. Confirm numbers. Confirm the ward's bed figures once you have checked them, so others can trust the count.
  4. Since you looked. What changed on this ward since you last looked.
  5. Contact and Staff needed. Contact opens the ward's contact lines. Staff needed records a request for extra staff.
  6. Checking. What the figures cannot vouch for, when the capacity was last confirmed, and whether the outage pack has been printed.
  7. Tonight's intake. The hours this ward takes admissions tonight. Edit sets them. It is in Checks and ward details.
  8. Ward tabs. Ward home, Referrals for this ward to answer, and Decisions.
  9. Find on this ward. Find a bed or a person on this ward.
  10. The toolbar. Open on TV opens a read only screen for the ward's TV. More, beside it, holds Notices, Shift brief and Ward handover.
  11. Place or move. Opens the bed board to place a patient in a bed or move one. The count says how many wait to be placed.
  12. Bed figures. Ready, pulled, closed and occupied beds, with when the ward last confirmed them.
  13. Coming and going. Arrivals today and the patients due to leave, past their date first.

On a phone

  • The round. Go through each patient in turn. The count shows how many are reviewed.
