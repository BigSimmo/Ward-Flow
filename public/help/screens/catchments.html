Catchments

BedChart · Which community team covers a suburb, every community team from A to Z, and team names that read alike.

Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

What is on the screen

  1. Catchments at a glance. How many community teams, suburbs mapped and team names that read alike.
  2. Suburb or postcode. Type a suburb, alias or postcode. The lookup below names the team, and the A to Z narrows to match.
  3. Catchment guide. Opens the guide to how catchments are set out.
  4. Shared suburbs. Shows only suburbs whose row names more than one team. Press again to show every suburb.
  5. Back to Places. Places lists every ward, ED and team. Catchments is this page.
  6. Suburb lookup. Names the team that covers the suburb, with Open and Show in list. The suburbs listed each name more than one team.
  7. A to Z. Every community team. Narrow by health service or jump to a letter, then open a team's own page.
  8. Reads like others. A team name close to another one. Press it to set them side by side and check you have the right team.
  9. Read alike. Team names that look similar, side by side. Choose a group, then open the right team.
  10. About this data. Where the team names and suburbs come from, and what they cannot tell you.

On a phone

  • One list at a time. Suburb, A to Z, Read alike or Services. Choose one to read that list.
