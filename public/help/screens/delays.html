What is on the screen

  1. Who is waiting. Everyone waiting in an ED or in transit, and the longest wait.
  2. Who the wait is on. Split by who can move it on next: you, the ED, the wards, transport or someone else.
  3. Saved views. Due soon or on you, Form due and For handover each narrow the list.
  4. The waiting list. Longest wait first. Open a row to deal with what is holding it up.

In Ward Flow, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.
