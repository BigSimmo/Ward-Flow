What is on the screen

  1. Who is waiting. Everyone waiting in an ED, those in transit, and the long waits. The long wait figures filter the list.
  2. Your move. How many are waiting on your move. Show these narrows the list to them, and Show everyone undoes it.
  3. Waiting tools. Find a bed opens the bed finder. The daily check and the next wait to cross its line show here when there are any.
  4. Who the wait is on. Split by who can move it on next: you, the ED, the wards, transport or someone else. Press one to show only theirs.
  5. Longest wait or By hold-up. Orders the list by the longest wait, or groups it by what is holding each person up.
  6. Saved views. Due soon or on you, Form due and For handover each narrow the list.
  7. The waiting list. Longest wait first. Open a row to deal with what is holding it up.
  8. Export. Saves the list as a spreadsheet.
  9. Today's follow-up. Attention, Escalated and Done today. Open a person to act or to read the reply.
  10. Delay graphs. The waits as graphs: the next hours, where and whose move, and the spread of waits.

In BedChart, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.
