Legal forms

Ward Flow · Every open movement with a legal form, and the expiry time a person typed from that form.

Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

What is on the screen

  1. Legal forms. Everyone on a form, how many are within your reminder window, and any with no time written. Times are as a person typed them.
  2. Act now. Forms whose typed time has passed or is within your reminder window. Open goes to the first one.
  3. Record a form. Opens Record a form, to record the time written on a new paper form.
  4. Record and tell. Forms in force by type, what is still to record on each form, and who to tell about recent moves.
  5. Copy for handover. Copies the form lines for your handover.
  6. On a form, Checklist and History. On a form lists everyone with a form now. Checklist shows what each form still needs. History holds closed moves. Seclusion and restraint, for the desks that see it, shows the seclusion and restraint forms.
  7. Print and Export. Print the list, or Export it as a spreadsheet.
  8. Choose a patient. Grouped by the typed expiry, earliest typed time first. Choose a patient to see their form.

Jobs done here

  • Record a new legal form. Open Legal forms and choose the patient, then Record next form. Or use Record a form at the top. Choose the patient and form type, type the time on the form, then Save form. Only the written time is recorded; no expiry is calculated.

In Ward Flow, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.
