What is on the screen

  1. Jobs open. How many transport jobs are open. The figures highlight jobs, such as those that need transport.
  2. Log booking. Records a booking already made by phone, with the CAD number and the quoted ETA.
  3. Needs transport. Beds pulled with no transport logged yet. Log booking on a row once it is booked.
  4. Jobs. Late or delayed jobs first, then forms to upload, then furthest along. History holds the jobs closed today.
  5. The next step. Each job's button is its next stage, such as En route or Arrived at ward. It is recorded with the time.

In Ward Flow, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.
