Discharges

Ward Flow · Planned discharges, held-up discharges first.

Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

What is on the screen

  1. Discharges today. How many to work now out of all open, dates overdue, and bed days past the ward's date.
  2. What to do next. The next job, such as chasing held-up discharges. Show narrows the list to them.
  3. Discharge actions. Ask wards about dates that have passed, Copy counts and Print list. On a phone, Per day and Filter sit here too.
  4. Held up by reason. What is holding discharges up, counted by reason. Press a reason to highlight its discharges.
  5. Now and History. Now lists the discharges still open. History lists those already recorded.
  6. Highlight by stage. Held up, confirmed and expected. Press one to highlight those rows; nobody is hidden.
  7. Held up first. Discharges with something in the way come first. Open one to see what is holding it up and which bed it frees.
  8. Export. Saves the list as a spreadsheet.

Jobs done here

  • Chase overdue discharge dates. On Discharges, press Ask N wards in the top: it sends only the wards with a date that has passed one desk alert asking them to confirm or record a new date. It reads Asked once all have been asked. To ask about one person, open them and use Message ward, then choose what to ask.
  • Clear a held-up discharge. Open Discharges, where held-up discharges come first. Choose the person: Held up says what is in the way, and Message ward asks the ward about it. Add what is outstanding under Discharge arrangements. On a phone, Clear hold-up removes it once it is sorted.
  • Track the arrangements a discharge needs. On Discharges, open the discharge; on a computer, choose its Held up tab. Under Discharge arrangements, choose the arrangement and who owns it, then Add as outstanding. Mark each one done, Not needed or Reconfirm as it moves. Nothing here frees the bed.

In Ward Flow, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.
