The weekly report

BedChart · One week's operations: the pilot measures, where the time goes and the pressure that week.

Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

What is on the screen

  1. The week at a glance. New long ED waits, out of area bed days, delayed discharge days and occupied bed time that week.
  2. Statistics views. Network, Compare, Services, Wards, EDs, Teams and Weekly. Each opens its own page.
  3. Choose the week. The last full week, this week so far, or on a computer one of the weeks before it.
  4. Pilot measures. The four agreed measures, such as referral to arrival. Targets are still proposals.
  5. Where the time goes. The typical time for each step finished that week, from referral to arrival.
  6. Supporting figures. Figures that help read the measures. They are not agreed measures.
  7. Pressure this week. Long ED waits, out of area days, delayed discharge days and occupied bed time that week.
  8. Delayed discharge by day. Bed days lost to delayed discharge, ward by day.
  9. Delayed days by reason. The same delayed bed days, by the reason recorded.
  10. Long ED waits. Each ED wait that first passed the target that week, and whether they are still waiting. Open Waiting for the list now.
  11. Declines and overrides. Placement and referral decisions that week, by reason. Switch between declines and overrides.
  12. Print. A paper copy of the week's report, with both declines and overrides.

In BedChart, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.
