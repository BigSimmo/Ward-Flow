Referrals

Ward Flow · Referrals waiting for an answer, and those decided recently.

Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

What is on the screen

  1. Referrals at a glance. How many are awaiting a decision, the oldest wait, and any decision overdue.
  2. Next overdue. The overdue referral that has waited longest. Next overdue opens it.
  3. Highlight referrals. Ward bed, ED, More than one and Refused once highlight those referrals in the queue.
  4. Bed meeting. Opens the bed meeting agenda at its referrals, the ones to talk through at the meeting.
  5. Since referral. Each referral's wait against its tier's decision window. Press a group to show those referrals.
  6. Beds ready. Beds ready now, by kind, so you can see what is free before asking a ward.
  7. Queue and History. Queue holds the referrals still to decide, History the decided ones. Export saves the list as a spreadsheet.
  8. Awaiting decision. Longest wait first, never ranked by the app. Choose a name to open the referral.
  9. Offered to wards. Referrals waiting on a ward's answer, with who was asked. Chase or Escalate a ward that is late to answer.

Jobs done here

  • Ask more wards. Open the referral. Wards asked shows each ward and its answer. Press Ask more wards, search or tick the wards, then press Ask. Wards are listed A to Z and never ranked, and only a ward that fits can be ticked. How many may wait at once is set by Wards asked at once in Settings.
  • Log a call. Open the referral. Under Log a call, choose Who was rung and What they said, then press Log a call. The next person sees who was called and when. A call does not answer the referral; the decision time keeps running.
  • Take a referral as your desk. Open the referral on Referrals, then its Patient tab (on a phone, Show patient details) and Owner and calls. The Owner line says who follows it up, or Not assigned. Press Take this referral. The Owner line then names your desk, with the time, so others know who is following it up.
  • Log a call. Open the referral on Referrals, then its Patient tab (on a phone, Show patient details) and Owner and calls. Under Log a call, choose who was rung and what they said, then press Log a call. The call is listed, newest first. A call does not answer the referral: the decision time keeps running.

In Ward Flow, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.
