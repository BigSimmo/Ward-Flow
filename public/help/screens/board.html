Bed board

BedChart · Every bed on one ward, who is in it, who is coming in and who is going out today and tomorrow.

Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

What is on the screen

  1. The ward now. Ready, occupied and pulled now, as at the time shown. Press Pulled or expected to see who they are.
  2. Worth a look. Show on board narrows the beds to the people worth a look, such as long stays and people waiting to leave.
  3. Shift brief and Change ward. Shift brief opens this ward's brief, ready to print. Change ward opens another ward's board.
  4. Also on this ward. Discharges confirmed or held up, people away at an ED, on leave or here over three months, and closed beds. Press a count to see who.
  5. Ward home and Bed board. Ward home is the ward's own page. Bed board is this one.
  6. Narrow the beds. All beds, Needs a look, Ready or No discharge due.
  7. Order the beds. Longest stay, Soonest to leave or Bed order.
  8. Every bed. One tile per bed, with the patient and their stay so far. Choose a bed to see who is in it and record a change.
  9. Long stays. Long stays and people ready to leave who are still here, with what they are waiting for.
  10. Tomorrow's expected beds. How many of this ward's discharges count in tomorrow's expected beds, confirmed or expected.
  11. Before handover. What needs you before the next handover. They have left, Record hold-up and Mark them back act on each row.
  12. Going out and coming in. Going out, Coming in and Since yesterday. Going out shows confirmed discharges today and tomorrow, or switch to expected.

On a phone

  • Beds as squares. One square per bed, with how many are ready and any closed.
