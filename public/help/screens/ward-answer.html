What is on the screen

  1. How full the ward is. The share of beds in use against the alert line, with the ward's occupied, ready, pulled and closed beds.
  2. One request at a time. Previous and Next step through the requests when more than one is waiting.
  3. The request. Who is asking for a bed, from which ED, with the safety tags the referral records.
  4. Current facts. Cohort, bed needed, sex, observation level and the other facts, as the referral records them.
  5. The referral. What the sender wrote, set out as situation, background, assessment and request.
  6. Your gates. Each gate reads Pass or Does not pass. They inform your answer and decide nothing.
  7. Decline. Opens the decline reasons. Choose one, then confirm the decline.
  8. Accept in principle. Opens Accept referral in principle to check and confirm. A person always chooses the bed.
  9. Confirm your beds. Set Free beds and press Confirm, so the network sees this ward's figure. It changes this ward only.
  10. Census. One square per bed: ready, pulled, closed or occupied.
  11. Recent answers. What this ward answered lately, and when.

In BedChart, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.
