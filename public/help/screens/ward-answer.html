What is on the screen

  1. The request. One bed request at a time. Previous and Next step through them when more than one is waiting.
  2. The referral. What the sender wrote, set out as situation, background, assessment and request.
  3. Your gates. Each gate reads Pass or Does not pass. They inform your answer and decide nothing.
  4. Confirm your beds. Confirm the ward's bed figures while you are here, so the network sees them.
  5. Recent answers. What this ward answered lately.

In Ward Flow, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.
