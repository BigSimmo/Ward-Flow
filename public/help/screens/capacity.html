Capacity

BedChart · Each ward's confirmed beds, what is ready, pulled or closed, and when it was last confirmed.

Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

What is on the screen

  1. How full the beds are. Ready beds, people who need a bed, how many short and the share in use, from each ward's last confirmed figures.
  2. Counts confirmed. How many wards confirmed their beds this shift, and the oldest count. Ask sends that ward a request to confirm.
  3. Bed meeting and TV. Bed meeting sheet opens the sheet in one tap. Open on TV is in the sheet's footer, when a TV is available, and puts the meeting on the room screen, read only. The arrow beside it, where shown, holds Open the meeting agenda.
  4. Mismatch. People waiting against beds that fit, for each kind of bed. Short means more people than beds. Press a bed type to see who is waiting and where.
  5. Overview and Wards. Overview shows the figures and Wards each ward's beds.
  6. Planned admissions. Admissions booked ahead, on the Planned tab. Press Record arrival when the person reaches the ward.
  7. Highlight wards. Has ready and Count old highlight wards on the map. Every ward stays.
  8. Find a bed. Opens the bed finder to see which wards have a bed ready of the kind needed. A person always chooses.
  9. Every ward. Each ward's beds, ready and being made ready, one square a bed. Choose a ward to open its panel.
  10. Held up. Discharges due by tomorrow that are held up, with what is holding each one.
  11. Expected from recorded dates. Tomorrow's spare and short beds by bed type, from the discharge dates wards recorded. Open shows the detail.

Jobs done here

  • See beds ready across the network. Open Capacity. It shows beds ready of the total, being made ready, in use and closed, then each health service, then Beds by ward grouped by service, with a row of bed squares. Bed counts only, with no patient details, as at the time shown.
  • Close a bed or a ward to admissions. On Capacity, open the ward, then press Close a bed or ward at the foot of its panel. To close a bed, choose a bed nobody is in and why, then Close bed. To close the ward, choose a reason under Close the whole ward to admissions and confirm. When ready, press Reopen ward in the same place.
  • Book a planned admission. On Capacity, open the Planned tab and press Book. Choose an existing patient or enter initials, age group and sex, then the reason, ward, day, arrival time, expected stay and legal status. Press Book. The booking shows in the list; its More menu holds Change booking and Cancel booking.
  • Reopen a closed ward to admissions. On Capacity, open the ward and press Reopen ward. Press Reopen to admissions and confirm. The ward is offered for new patients again straight away. Anything still pointed at it is listed so you can mark each one dealt with.
  • Ask a ward to confirm its bed numbers. On Capacity, open the ward and press Ask the ward. On Places, press Ask to confirm. The ward is asked to confirm its beds. Capacity then reads Update requested, and Places reads Asked, with the time. The ward confirms on its own home.

In BedChart, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.
