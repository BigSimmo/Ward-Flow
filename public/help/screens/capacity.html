Capacity

Ward Flow · Each ward's confirmed beds, what is ready, pulled or closed, and when it was last confirmed.

Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

What is on the screen

  1. How full the beds are. The share of beds in use across what your desk can see, from each ward's last confirmed figures.
  2. Mismatch. People waiting against beds that fit, for each kind of bed. Short means more people than beds.
  3. Planned admissions. Admissions booked ahead. Press Arrived when the person reaches the ward.
  4. Nearest ready beds. Ready beds by road distance from the place you choose. Distance only, not suitability.
  5. Every ward. Each ward's beds, ready and being made ready. Choose a ward to open its panel.
  6. Bed meeting and TV. Bed meeting opens a menu: Print the meeting sheet and Open the meeting agenda. Open on TV shows only when a TV is available, and puts the meeting on the room screen, read only.

Jobs done here

  • See beds ready across the network. Open Capacity. It shows beds ready of the total, then Beds by ward: beds, ready, being made ready, in use and closed. Bed counts only, with no patient details, as at the time shown.
  • Close a bed or a ward to admissions. On Capacity, open the ward, then press Closures at the foot of its panel. Choose a bed nobody is in and a reason to close it, or close the whole ward to admissions with a reason. A closed bed or ward is not offered until someone reopens it there.
  • Book a planned admission. On Capacity, under Planned moves, press Book. Choose an existing patient or enter initials, age group and sex, then the reason, ward, day, arrival time, expected stay and legal status. Press Book. Change or cancel it from its row.
  • Reopen a closed ward to admissions. On Capacity, open the ward and press Closures. Press Reopen to admissions and confirm. The ward is offered for new patients again straight away. Anything still pointed at it is listed so you can mark each one dealt with.
  • Ask a ward to confirm its bed numbers. On Capacity, open the ward and press Ask the ward. On Places, press Ask to confirm. The ward is asked to confirm its beds, and the status reads Asked with the time.

In Ward Flow, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.
