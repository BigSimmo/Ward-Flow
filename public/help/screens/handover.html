Handover

Ward Flow · A point-in-time handover sheet for the next shift, ready to print.

Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

What is on the screen

  1. What to hand over. Act now, flagged urgent and due before the handover. Read Act now first.
  2. Ward promises to chase. Times a ward said it would ring back. Chase the overdue ones first.
  3. The handover sheet. Use Show to filter the sheet to a service, then use Print handover for the paper copy.
  4. Sign off. Sign off shows how many checks are clear. Open it, work through the Before you sign checks, then choose Sign off.

Jobs done here

  • Hand over to the next shift. Open Handover. Read Act now first, then what is due before the next handover. Work through Before you sign, then choose Sign off.
  • Print the handover sheet. Open Handover and choose Print. Present opens the meeting screen.
  • What to do if Ward Flow is down. Switch to the paper process. The downtime pack on Handover prints what you need. The app shows when it last saved, and says so when it is offline.

In Ward Flow, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.
