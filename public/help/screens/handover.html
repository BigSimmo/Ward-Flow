Handover

Ward Flow · A point-in-time handover sheet for the next shift, ready to print.

Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

What is on the screen

  1. Which handover. The handover time and shift this sheet is for. Choose it to switch to another handover.
  2. What to hand over. Act now, flagged urgent and due before the handover. Read Act now first. Each figure highlights its rows.
  3. Since the last handover. What is new since the last handover, such as new referrals and moves.
  4. Ward replies due. Times a ward said it would ring back. Chase the overdue ones first.
  5. Start meeting. Starts the meeting clock. Press it again to stop; History records how long the meeting took.
  6. Digest. The shift digest: a short summary of this shift to read or copy.
  7. Sign off. Sign off shows how many checks are clear. Open it, work through the Before you sign checks, then choose Sign off.
  8. Patients, Beds and History. Patients is the handover sheet. Beds lists the beds ready, and History the handovers signed before.
  9. Set up the sheet. Show filters the sheet to a ward or service. Detail sets the columns, and the other controls highlight or arrange rows.
  10. The handover sheet. One row a patient, grouped as you chose. Act now and urgent rows stand out.
  11. Print handover. Opens the paper copy of the sheet to print.
  12. More. Present on a screen, Copy summary, the Downtime pack and Open in Waiting.

On a phone

  • Each patient. Open a patient for their handover details.

Jobs done here

  • Hand over to the next shift. Open Handover. Read Act now first, then what is due before the next handover. Work through Before you sign: each line has its own button where something needs doing. Then press Sign off. Undo sign-off takes it back if it was a mistake.
  • Print the handover sheet. Open Handover and choose Print handover. Choose what to include, the paper (A4 landscape or portrait) and any highlight, review the pages, then press Print or save PDF. Back to handover returns without printing.
  • What to do if Ward Flow is down. Switch to the paper process. The downtime pack on Handover prints what you need; print it before you need it. The app shows when it last saved, and says so when it is offline.
  • Sign off the site handover. On Handover, the Site handover card shows each ward's sign-off, read only, and the checks before you sign, each with a link to where it is done. Press Sign off at (the time shown) when the checks allow. Undo sign-off shows afterwards.
  • Undo a handover sign-off. On Handover, open the sign-off panel; once signed it reads Signed off at, with the time. Press Undo sign-off. A site bed manager presses Undo sign-off on the Site handover card. A coordinator who signed as cover cannot undo a site sign-off.

In Ward Flow, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.
