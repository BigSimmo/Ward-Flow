All wards

BedChart · Every ward, grouped by health service, each opening its own ward screen.

Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

What is on the screen

  1. Beds ready now. Ready beds across the wards you can see, those expected to leave today, and how many are short or full.
  2. Has ready. Highlights the wards with a bed ready. Press it again to show every ward the same.
  3. Counts confirmed. How many wards confirmed their beds this shift, and the oldest count. Ask sends that ward a request to confirm.
  4. In use by service. The share in use and beds ready for each health service. Press a service to highlight its wards.
  5. Services. All, or one health service's wards.
  6. Filters. Search by ward, hospital or service, and choose status, cohort and order.
  7. Table view. Shows the wards as a table instead of cards. Card view switches back.
  8. Reading a ward. One square a bed. The key says what each colour means.
  9. Open a ward. Each ward with its beds. Open one for its beds, arrivals and discharges.
  10. Counts to check. The wards whose bed count is past its limit, so you know who to ask.

Jobs done here

  • Pin a ward to my menu. Open the ward, then in the side menu choose Pin this ward. It stays under My wards on this device.

In BedChart, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.
