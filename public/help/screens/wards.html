Ward Flow · Every ward, grouped by health service, each opening its own ward screen.

What is on the screen

  1. Beds ready now. Ready beds across the wards you can see, and the share in use. The counts beside it narrow the list, such as Stale counts.
  2. Find a ward. Search by ward, hospital or service. Filters holds status, age group and order.
  3. Open a ward. Each ward with its beds. Open one for its beds, arrivals and discharges.
  4. By service. Occupancy for each health service, and the wards whose bed count needs a check.

Jobs done here

  • Pin a ward to my menu. Open the ward, then in the side menu choose Pin this ward. It stays under My wards on this device.

In Ward Flow, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.
