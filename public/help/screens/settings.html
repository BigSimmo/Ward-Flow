What is on the screen

  1. Find a setting. Type what you want to change, such as theme or alerts, and choose it to go straight there.
  2. Alerts on this device. A warning mark on the Alerts tab, or Off on a phone, means alerts do not reach this device. Open Alerts to turn them on.
  3. Where the rules stand. Whether the rules are at default, the last save, and that rule changes wait for Save.
  4. Sections. Each desk sees its own sections, such as Rules, Alerts, Display and Your desk.
  5. Changed from default. Shows only the rules that differ from the default. It appears once a rule has changed.
  6. Standard or Surge. Switches the timing set. The line under it lists each rule Surge changes.
  7. Rule values. Each rule shows its range. Use minus and plus to change it.
  8. Save rule changes. Rule changes wait here until you Save, and every save is recorded. Discard drops them.
  9. Rules file and tables. Save or load the rules as a file, and read the published reference tables.
  10. Will alerts reach you. Alerts lists each way an alert can reach this device and which are ready, with quiet hours and the buzz sound.
  11. Display. Theme, row density and the other choices for this device. They save at once.
  12. Your desk. What your screens include, the service in view and the shift you are on.
  13. Demo tools. Demo controls, Reset for this browser and Copy for diagnostics. For the demo only.

On a phone

  • Areas. Each area opens in its own sheet. A count on Rules shows changes not yet saved.
