The agenda

Ward Flow · The bed meeting's agenda, built from fixed rules, and the actions agreed at earlier meetings.

Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

What is on the screen

  1. The lists at a glance. How many are on each list. Press a figure to show only that list, and again to show every list.
  2. Earlier actions. Actions from earlier meetings, with how many are overdue, still open and done in the last day.
  3. Find anyone. Opens a search to find anyone to discuss by name or UMRN.
  4. Referrals. Shows only referrals awaiting a decision. Press again to show every list.
  5. Run meeting. Steps the meeting through each service in turn, with Next and Stop, then a summary of what was recorded.
  6. Filter by group. Shows only the groups you choose. Each one shows above the lists, with Clear.
  7. TV or printed sheet. Open on TV shows the meeting room view. Bed meeting sheet has the printable sheet.
  8. Service. Statewide or one health service, so the meeting can go service by service.
  9. Actions from earlier meetings. Each open action with its owner and due time. Mark done closes one once it is.
  10. The lists. Longest ED waits, pulled beds soonest to expire, stranded stays, out of area and closed beds past review.
  11. Add an action. Action on a row records what was agreed for that person, with an owner and when it is due.
  12. Referrals awaiting decision. Referrals still waiting for a decision. Copy puts the list on the clipboard.

In Ward Flow, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.
