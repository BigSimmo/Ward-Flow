The agenda

Ward Flow · The bed meeting's agenda, built from fixed rules, and the actions agreed at earlier meetings.

Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

What is on the screen

  1. Filter the agenda. Choose a service, pick which lists to show, or find anyone to discuss.
  2. Earlier actions. Actions agreed at earlier meetings that are still open. Mark done closes one once it is.
  3. The lists. Longest ED waits, pulled beds with least time left, stranded stays, out of area and referrals awaiting a decision.
  4. Add an action. Action against a person records what was agreed, with an owner and a due time.
  5. Start meeting. Opens the meeting room view. Bed meeting sheet has the printable sheet.

In Ward Flow, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.
