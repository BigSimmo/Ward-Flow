Alerts

Ward Flow · What is addressed to this desk right now, across every movement and referral.

Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

What is on the screen

  1. Addressed to your desk. Alerts for this desk right now, act now first.
  2. Show open alerts. Act now, Waiting, Forecast, Reminders, Notices, Snoozed or Yours. All shows every one again.
  3. Open an alert. Each alert opens the record it is about.
  4. The order. How the list is ordered: act now first, then waiting, oldest first in each.
  5. Forecast. Forecast lines set in Settings: beds likely to be short tomorrow, the ED access target and a worse forecast.

Jobs done here

  • Find an alert I already cleared. Open the Alerts page. Open is what still needs someone, with chips to show one kind at a time. Cleared lists what was acted on this session, newest first. Broadcasts holds bed calls, live and past.
  • Send a bed call or broadcast alert. On Alerts, press Broadcast alert. Choose Ask wards for beds, or Tell desks something. Choose who gets it, check What each desk sees, then send. Wards answer Can take, After a discharge or Can't.
  • Extend or stand down a broadcast alert. On the banner, or Alerts then Broadcasts, press Extend 30m for half an hour more. Press Stand down to end it; it asks you to confirm first.
  • Add desks to a broadcast alert. Open Alerts, choose Broadcasts, then press Add desks on the live alert. Tick the desks to add and press Add. The button is greyed when every desk is already asked.
  • Take an alert as mine, or hand it back. Open the alert from Alerts, or the Alerts bell at the top, and press Take; it then reads Yours. To give it back, press Hand back and confirm. The alert returns to the queue and Undo shows for a short while.
  • Snooze or acknowledge an alert. Open the alert, then More actions. Snooze asks how long and why. Acknowledge marks it Seen and leaves it on watch. Return now brings a snoozed alert back. Act now alerts can only be snoozed for a short time.

In Ward Flow, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.
