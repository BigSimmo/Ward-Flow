Alerts

BedChart · What is addressed to this desk right now, across every movement and referral.

Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

What is on the screen

  1. Addressed to your desk. Alerts for this desk right now: act now, waiting, and how old the oldest is.
  2. Next alert. The alert to deal with next. Next alert opens it.
  3. Broadcast alert. Sends one alert to several desks.
  4. Today. Alerts raised and acted on today, what is being checked, and any broadcasts.
  5. Open, Cleared and Broadcasts. Open alerts, the ones cleared, and the broadcasts sent.
  6. Show open alerts. Act now, Waiting, Notices or Yours. All shows every one again.
  7. Group by. Urgency lists act now first. Owner groups the alerts by who has them.
  8. Open an alert. Each alert opens the record it is about.
  9. Expected from recorded dates. Lines set in Settings: beds expected to be short tomorrow, the ED access target and expected beds that got worse.
  10. Notices. For information only, such as an arrival running late.
  11. The order. How the list is ordered: act now first, then waiting, oldest first in each.

Jobs done here

  • Find an alert I already cleared. Open the Alerts page. Open is what still needs someone, with chips to show one kind at a time. Cleared lists what was acted on this session, newest first. Broadcasts holds bed calls, live and past.
  • Send a bed call or broadcast alert. On Alerts, press Broadcast alert. Choose Ask wards for beds or Tell desks something, then who gets it, how urgent, a title and message, and when it ends. Check What each desk sees, tick I'm authorised, then Send. Wards answer Can take, After a discharge or Can't.
  • Extend or stand down a broadcast alert. On the banner, or Alerts then Broadcasts, press Extend 30m for half an hour more. Press Stand down to end it; it asks you to confirm first. The time left shows on the banner and the live card.
  • Add desks to a broadcast alert. Open Alerts, choose Broadcasts, then press Add desks on the live alert. Tick the desks to ask, or All for a group, and press Add, which names how many. The button is greyed when every desk is already asked.
  • Take an alert as mine, or hand it back. Open the alert from Alerts, or the Alerts bell at the top, and press Take; it then reads Yours. To give it back, press Hand back and confirm. The alert returns to the queue and Undo shows for a short while. Yours at the top of Alerts counts the alerts you own.
  • Snooze or acknowledge an alert. Open the alert, then More actions. Snooze asks when it should come back and why. Acknowledge marks it Seen and leaves it on watch. Return now brings a snoozed alert back. Act now alerts can only be snoozed for a short time.
