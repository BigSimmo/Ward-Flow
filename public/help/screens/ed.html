What is on the screen

  1. Each ED. Every emergency department, with the people there waiting for a mental health bed.
  2. Filter and order. Filter by health service or status, and choose the order.
  3. Preview who is waiting. Choose an ED's card to preview who is waiting there without leaving the list.
  4. Enter an ED. Enter opens that ED's board and each patient's next step.

On a phone

  • Open an ED. Each row is one ED, with how many are waiting. Open it for that ED's board.

Jobs done here

  • Record the arrival checks for the receiving ward. Open the person on the ED board. The Arrival checklist shows how many of the two checks are recorded. Press Record done for each; Take back undoes one.
  • Record that no transport is needed. On the ED board, open the row's actions and press Record no transport needed.
  • Record a medical deterioration after clearance. Open the person on the ED board and press Record medical deterioration, then confirm. It cancels the allocation, releases the bed, cancels transport not yet collected and pauses placement until cleared again.

In Ward Flow, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.
