Out of area

Ward Flow · Patients in a bed outside their home health service, with days away and any return plan.

Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

What is on the screen

  1. Away from home. How many people are in a bed outside their home health service, split by days away. Checking counts discharge dates passed and missing return plans.
  2. Quick views. Discharge date passed, no plan and the long trips home. Each narrows the list.
  3. The list. Each person's home region, placement and discharge date.
  4. Return plan. Choose a person and their return plan opens in a side panel.

Jobs done here

  • Plan a patient's return home. Open Out of area and choose the person. Fill in their return plan, then Record sent to ward or Record return.

In Ward Flow, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.
