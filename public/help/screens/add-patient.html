Add patient

Ward Flow · Adds a new invented patient record. Only the core details are needed.

Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

What is on the screen

  1. Core details. Family name, given name, date of birth and UMRN. Everything else is optional.
  2. Duplicate check. Checks every record as you type, so one person does not get two records.
  3. More details. Contact and address, care links, and culture and language. All optional.
  4. Add patient. Opens the new record. Add and start referral goes straight to a referral instead.

In Ward Flow, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.

Prototype on synthetic data. Not for real patients.
