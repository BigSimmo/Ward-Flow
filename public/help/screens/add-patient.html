Add patient

BedChart · Adds a new invented patient record. Only the core details are needed.

Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

What is on the screen

  1. A draft until added. Nothing is saved until you add the patient. Cancel goes back to patient search without adding anyone.
  2. Core details. Family name, given name, date of birth and UMRN. The count shows how many of the four are done.
  3. Sex. Female, Male, Another term or Not recorded.
  4. Duplicate check. Checks every record as you type a UMRN or a name of four letters or more, so one person does not get two records.
  5. Open all. Opens every optional section at once. Close all folds them again.
  6. Optional details. Gender, contact and address, next of kin and carer, Medicare, GP and community team, culture and language, and planning.
  7. What is still needed. Names the core details still blank. Reset clears every field, and Undo puts them back.
  8. Add and start referral. Adds the patient and goes straight to a new referral for them.
  9. Add patient. Adds the patient and opens their new record.
  10. Recently added. Records added lately, with how many were added today.

In BedChart, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.
