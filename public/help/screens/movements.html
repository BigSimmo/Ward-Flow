Ward Flow · Every patient moving to a bed, by stage, from referral to arrival.

Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

What is on the screen

  1. Open movements. Everyone moving to a bed now, from referral to arrival.
  2. The timeline. What is coming up for each receiving ward.
  3. Open a movement. Movements are listed by stage. Open one to see its bed, transport and legal form in one place.

On a phone

  • Filter. Narrow the list to the movements you are looking for.

Jobs done here

  • Pull the bed for a patient. Once a ward accepts, open the movement and press Pull the bed. The bed is kept for the patient within the pull time limit. A person always chooses the bed; nothing is placed automatically.
  • Book transport. On the movement, press Book transport, or record that none is needed. The app records the booking for the transport officer; it does not order the vehicle itself.
  • Book transport. Open the patient from Coming and going, then use Record transport booking on the journey. You may record a booking for a journey your ward sent. Ward Flow records the booking; it does not order a vehicle.
  • Cancel transport. On Movements, open a referral your ED sent. Under Transport booked with no bed pulled, choose a reason, then Cancel the transport job. This control handles orphaned jobs only; ask the coordinator about ordinary bookings. No cancellation is possible after collection.
  • Cancel transport. On Coming and going, open the movement. Under Transport booked with no bed pulled, choose a reason, then Cancel the transport job. Only the sending ward recorded as booker may cancel; receiving the patient is not enough. Ask the coordinator about ordinary bookings. No cancellation is possible after collection.
  • Cancel transport. Open the movement and cancel the booked transport there. Do this before you release a pulled bed, so the officer is not sent for a patient whose bed has gone.
