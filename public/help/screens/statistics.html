Statistics

Ward Flow · Counts and waits for the network, a service or a ward, worked out from the records.

Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

What is on the screen

  1. Choose a view. Network, Compare, Services, Wards, EDs, Teams and Weekly. Every figure is from synthetic records.
  2. Occupancy by service. Beds in use for each health service.
  3. Ask a question. Fixed questions answered from the records, such as which pulled beds are past their pull time limit.
  4. Export the figures. Downloads the figures at the top of the page as a spreadsheet file.

On a phone

  • More figures. Discharges held up, declines by reason and the other figures.

Jobs done here

  • See the statistics. Open Statistics for the network's trends. Every figure is from synthetic records.
  • Compare wards in the statistics. Open Statistics, then Compare. Pin up to three wards to set them beside the network, or choose two wards to compare. Every figure is from synthetic records.
  • Read the weekly report. Open Statistics, then Weekly. The pilot's four agreed measures lead, and the two counted outside Ward Flow say so. How this is counted explains each one.
