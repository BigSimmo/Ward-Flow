Statistics

BedChart · Counts and waits for the network, a service or a ward, worked out from the records.

Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

What is on the screen

  1. Choose a view. Network, Compare, Services, Wards, EDs, Teams and Weekly. Every figure is from synthetic records.
  2. Network at a glance. Beds in use, ED journeys open, discharges today and those expected to leave today.
  3. Wards at the line. How many wards are at or over the bed alert line. Show wards takes you to them.
  4. Open a unit. Open a unit finds any ward, ED, service or team and opens its figures.
  5. Export the figures. Downloads the figures at the top of the page as a spreadsheet file. On a phone it is in the … menu.
  6. Bed board, Movement or Map. Shows the network as a bed board, as the movement of people, or on a map.
  7. Occupancy by service. Beds in use for each health service.
  8. Ask a question. Fixed questions answered from the records, such as which pulled beds are past their pull time limit.

On a phone

  • More figures. Discharges held up, declines by reason and the other figures.

Jobs done here

  • See the statistics. Open Statistics for the network's trends. Choose a view: Network, Compare, Services, Wards, EDs, Teams or Weekly. On a computer, Export downloads the figures at the top of the page. Every figure is from synthetic records.
  • Compare wards in the statistics. Open Statistics, then Compare. On a computer, choose a measure for the ward and ED charts, and Pin a ward to set up to three beside the network. On a phone, choose Wards or EDs and a measure, then Compare two wards. Every figure is from synthetic records.
  • Read the weekly report. Open Statistics, then Weekly. Choose the week, then read Pilot measures (the four agreed measures; the two counted outside BedChart say so), Where the time goes, Supporting figures and Pressure this week. How this is counted explains each one. Print prints the report.

In BedChart, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.
