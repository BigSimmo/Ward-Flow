What is on the screen

  1. What needs a check. The band names the wards whose bed count is old, with the ward to ask first.
  2. Places and Catchments. Catchments finds which community team covers a suburb.
  3. Search places. Type a ward, hospital, ED, team or service. Show narrows the list to wards, EDs or teams.
  4. The network. Every place, grouped by service. Choose one to see who is heading or waiting there, then refer or open it.
  5. At a glance. Opens wards that need a bed check, wards by service, and the places you pinned or opened lately.
