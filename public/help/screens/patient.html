Patient record

BedChart · One patient's record: where they are now, their movement and the next step.

Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

What is on the screen

  1. Who and where. Name, record number and where they are now, such as In ED or Inpatient.
  2. At a glance. Legal status, observation level, risk and alerts, as recorded. Legal opens their forms.
  3. Copy, Pin and Forms. Copy handover or Copy summary copies a short summary. Pin keeps the record on Home. Forms opens their legal forms.
  4. Where they are up to. Each step from the ED to the community, with the current one marked and the dates recorded.
  5. The record. Now, History, Details and Documents (Docs on a phone). History lists each change as it was saved.
  6. The status card. Where they stand now. In an ED: the bed, fit to travel and transport, each with who does it. On a ward: their day of stay.
  7. Destination or a change. In an ED, Destination asks wards, pulls the bed or corrects it. On a ward, Record a change starts leave, Gone to ED, a discharge date or absence.
  8. Legal now. The legal forms in force, as recorded. Forms or Documents lists every form.
  9. Discharge readiness. On a ward: the discharge date, community team and next contact, as recorded. The Discharge step shows the same date.
  10. Triage and risk. The signed triage on record. Triage opens the triage form.
  11. Carer call back. Records that a carer wants a call back and when, then Call made once it is done.
  12. Care and safety. Add care plan, the tribunal hearing date, whether a carer was told and cultural support asked for.
  13. Who to call. The ED or ward caring for them now, with a number to copy. Numbers are synthetic.

In BedChart, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.
