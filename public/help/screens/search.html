Patients

BedChart · The register of everyone, found by record number, place, stage or owner.

Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

What is on the screen

  1. Find a person. Search by name, UMRN or place.
  2. Add patient. Register a new synthetic person when they are not found.
  3. Today. How many arrived, left or did not proceed today.
  4. Where people are now. Everyone, In ED, In placement, Inpatient, Community or Not active, with how many in each.
  5. This shift. What happened this shift, such as a pull that expired, with the time and the person.
  6. Everyone now. Open movements and stays. Open a row for the person's details.
  7. Sort and highlight. Sort by longest wait, tier or name. Highlight marks rows without hiding anyone.
  8. Copy list and Export. Copy list copies the rows as text. Export saves them as a spreadsheet.

On a phone

  • Highlight. Lights the people who match, such as Tier 1, without hiding anyone.

Jobs done here

  • Remove a patient record added in error. Open the patient, then their Details tab, and find the Record added in error card. Choose why it should go, press Archive this record, then Archive record to confirm. The record leaves every list and only the audit trail shows it. Undo shows straight after.

In BedChart, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.
