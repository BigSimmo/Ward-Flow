BedChart · Recorded decisions to review, gaps by site and the audit trail for this session.

Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

What is on the screen

  1. What to review. Recorded decisions waiting for review, such as overrides and refusals, for this session.
  2. Newest to review. The newest event waiting for review. Review next opens it.
  3. Record review. Opens Record a review: choose what is under review and the decision, then save it.
  4. Governance actions. PIR chronology opens the patient chronology report. Repeat break glass highlights repeated openings when there are any.
  5. Views. Review, Forms to chase, Audit trail, Registers and Reports.
  6. Show. All, Overrides, Steps not saved, or Record access.
  7. Waiting for review. Newest first. Choose an event and its detail opens beside the list.

On a phone

  • Waiting for review. Newest first. Choose an event, then Record review.

Jobs done here

  • Look through the audit trail. Open Governance. Review lists events waiting for review, newest first: overrides, steps not saved and record access. Select one to see its facts, then press Reviewed, or Follow-up if it needs more. Audit trail lists what has been reviewed.

In BedChart, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.
