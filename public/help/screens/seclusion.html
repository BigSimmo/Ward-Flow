Seclusion

Ward Flow · An operational view of seclusion rooms, seclusion and restraint in use, and incidents, ward by ward.

Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

What is on the screen

  1. In use now. Everyone in seclusion or restraint now, longest first. Ward staff record changes on their own ward.
  2. Start seclusion or restraint. Find the patient, choose what is in use, type the time or press Now, then Record start.
  3. The seclusion rooms. Each secure ward's room. Change marks it available or out of service.
  4. Record incident. Choose the ward and the kind, type the time, then Record incident.

Jobs done here

  • Record seclusion, restraint or an incident. Open Forms, then Seclusion and restraint. Start seclusion or restraint when it begins and Record end when it finishes, or Record incident for an incident.
  • Mark the seclusion room available or out of service. Open Seclusion and choose the ward. Under Seclusion room, press Mark available or Mark out of service, then confirm. Everyone sees the status until it is marked again.
  • Record the end of seclusion or restraint. Open Seclusion and restraint. On the live episode, press Record end, type the time it ended and press Save end time.

In Ward Flow, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.
