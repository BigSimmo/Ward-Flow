Seclusion

Ward Flow · An operational view of seclusion rooms, seclusion and restraint in use, and incidents, ward by ward.

Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

What is on the screen

  1. An operational view. Times are as typed, and nothing works out a legal limit.
  2. Ward by ward. Each ward's seclusion room, what is in use now and its incidents. Choose a ward.
  3. The seclusion room. Mark available or Mark out of service for the chosen ward's room.
  4. Record start and end. Choose the patient and what is in use, type the time, then Record start. Record end when it finishes.
  5. Record incident. Choose the kind, type the time, then Record incident.

Jobs done here

  • Record seclusion, restraint or an incident. Open Forms, then Seclusion and restraint. Start seclusion or restraint when it begins and Record end when it finishes, or Record incident for an incident. Times are as typed, and nothing works out a legal limit.

In Ward Flow, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.
