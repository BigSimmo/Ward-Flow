What is on the screen

  1. How full the network is. The share of network beds in use, with At risk once it passes the line. Open it for Capacity.
  2. Every ED at a glance. One tile per emergency department, with how many are waiting and the longest wait. Choose a tile to narrow the list to that ED, and again to clear it.
  3. Needs you and Flow. Needs you is what needs a person. Flow lists every open movement, and its tabs narrow it by stage.
  4. Needs you. Act now first, then referrals to decide and what you are waiting on others for. Choosing a row opens its placement checks beside the list; a person always chooses the bed.
  5. Beds that fit. For each bed type, beds against the people waiting, now and tomorrow. Short means more people than beds. Counts only.

In Ward Flow, open Help and choose Show me around this screen to see each part lit in turn.
