Ward Flow · My jobs

Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

Jobs you do most

  1. Open a job. Choose a job to see the booking, the forms and the arrival plan.
  2. Move it along. Press Accepted, then En route, Collected and Arrived at ward as each step happens.
  3. Undo a slip. If you pressed the wrong step, press Undo on the message straight away.

What you cannot do

  • You see only your own service's jobs.
  • You cannot accept a referral, pull a bed or change a ward's beds.
  • A step can be refused, for example when a legal order changed. The message says why.

Colour code

  • Triangle: act now.
  • Filled amber circle: at risk.
  • Tick: done.
  • Capsule: moving.
  • Ring: waiting.
  • Cross: declined or closed.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • /: Search, from the box in the header. On Settings it finds a setting.
  • Ctrl+K: Search, even with single-key shortcuts off. Cmd+K on a Mac.
  • Shift+T: Open Alerts, called Tasks on some desks.
  • Shift+A: Open Activity.
  • [: Fold the side menu to icons, or open it again.
  • Esc: Close what is open, or step back.
  • ?: Show these shortcuts. On Handover and Referrals, list that page's own keys.
