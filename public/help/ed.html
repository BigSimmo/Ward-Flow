Print on A4 at 100%. Use your browser’s Print command.

Jobs you do most

  1. Raise a referral. Use New referral, or Raise referral on the board. Choose one or more destinations and send it.
  2. Record ED steps. On a patient, use Record examination, Record medically cleared and Mark handover ready.
  3. Legal paperwork. Use Change legal status and Mark Form 1A received. Type any expiry time from the paper form.

What you cannot do

  • The board never re-sorts the queue. It stays longest wait first.
  • You cannot accept for a ward or place a patient.
  • The app never works out Mental Health Act times. It shows only times someone typed from the paper form.

Colour code

  • Triangle: act now.
  • Filled amber circle: at risk.
  • Tick: done.
  • Capsule: moving.
  • Ring: waiting.
  • Cross: declined or closed.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • /: Search, from the box in the header. On Settings it finds a setting.
  • Ctrl+K: Search, even with single-key shortcuts off. Cmd+K on a Mac.
  • Shift+T: Open Alerts, called Tasks on some desks.
  • Shift+A: Open Activity.
  • Shift+R: Start a new referral.
  • [: Fold the side menu to icons, or open it again.
  • Alt+N: Jump to the newest notice's button, and back. Option+N on a Mac.
  • Esc: Close what is open, or step back.
  • ?: Show these shortcuts. On Handover and Referrals, list that page's own keys.
