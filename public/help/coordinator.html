Jobs you do most

  1. Decide a referral. Press Decide, read the referral, then Accept referral or Decline referral. A decline needs a reason.
  2. Ask more wards. Use Ask more wards and tick the wards. Wards stay in a fixed order; the app never ranks or suggests one.
  3. Pull the bed. Open the patient from Movements. Once a ward accepts, press Pull the bed. It is kept for the patient within the pull time limit.

What you cannot do

  • A person always chooses the bed. Nothing is placed automatically.
  • Wait lists stay in wait order. Flags highlight a row but never move it.
  • The app never works out Mental Health Act times. It shows only times someone typed from the paper form.

Colour code

  • Triangle: act now.
  • Filled amber circle: at risk.
  • Tick: done.
  • Capsule: moving.
  • Ring: waiting.
  • Cross: declined or closed.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • /: Search, from the box in the header. On Settings it finds a setting.
  • Ctrl+K: Search, even with single-key shortcuts off. Cmd+K on a Mac.
  • Shift+T: Open Alerts, called Tasks on some desks.
  • Shift+A: Open Activity.
  • Shift+R: Start a new referral.
  • [: Fold the side menu to icons, or open it again.
  • Alt+N: Jump to the newest notice's button, and back. Option+N on a Mac.
  • Esc: Close what is open, or step back.
  • ?: Show these shortcuts. On Handover and Referrals, list that page's own keys.
