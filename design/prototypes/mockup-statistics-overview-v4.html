What is real, taken from the repository

  • 23 units across 15 hospitals — 16 Adult, 6 Older adult, 1 Youth — from ward-sites.ts. EMHS, SMHS, NMHS and WACHS hold 21; St John of God Subiaco's 2 are private capacity, its own row.
  • Graylands (North Metro's Older Adult unit) is genuinely stale in the source model — confirmed over 3 hours past its window. That's why its Ready count reads Unknown, not a number.
  • The seven movement stages, and the ruled distinction between “no bed available” and “not a match for any bed”, are the domain model this page is built to.
  • The visual language above the marker — colours, spacing, panels, tables, chips, rings, charts — is statistics-language-v4.css, copied unedited, mirroring the app's ckb-v2-tokens.css.

What is invented

  • Every count on this page — the 47 waiting, the seven stage counts and medians, WF-013's 31-hour wait, every Ready-bed count except the stale one, and today's referral, placement and arrival figures.
  • The per-service split, and the in-area/rate breakdown behind its disclosure, reuse the same totals shown elsewhere (27 raised, 19 placed, 7 out of area, 8 still open) — not a second data set.
  • Every sparkline's shape is illustrative movement, not a recorded series.
  • The “confirmed 10:42 by 14 of 15 hospitals” liveness marker at the top of the page.

What this screen deliberately does not do

  • No switcher, no action that changes a record — the top of the hierarchy every switcher routes out of. A built version would link each figure onward, not act here.
  • “No bed available” and “not a match for any bed” stay separate — different problems, different fixes, never read as one cause.
  • Every zero is real and measured. A missing unit says so in words, not a zero. An unconfirmed figure says “unknown”, never its last known number.
  • It draws two charts, not the third edition's several: the stage line and the Ready ring. Every other proportion is an exact number, in a table or behind a disclosure.
