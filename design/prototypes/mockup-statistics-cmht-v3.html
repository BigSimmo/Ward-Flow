How the numbers agree with each other

  • 168 people currently open to the team = 44 open under 3 months + 58 at 3 months–1 year + 46 at 1–3 years + 20 over 3 years.
  • 24 new cases opened this month minus 17 cases closed this month = a net change of +7, the same +7 the centrepiece chart shows as August’s gap.
  • The 3 hospital beds waiting on this team = 9 + 5 + 2 days each, waited so far, summing to 16 bed-days currently owed. The longest, 9 days, is the single wait named at the top of the page.
  • 36 referrals received this month = 24 accepted + 6 declined + 6 still open. The 24 accepted are the same 24 counted in this month’s 24 new cases opened. 3 of the 6 still open are the 3 beds waiting on this team above.
  • The 6 declined referrals = 4 declined for want of capacity + 1 outside the catchment area + 1 outside this team’s age cohort, and the last two sum to the 2 declined as not this team’s catchment or cohort. These two totals are never added into one figure on this page.
  • The 36 referrals received, by source, = 11 ward discharge + 8 emergency department + 10 GP + 5 self or family + 2 another community team.
  • The same 36 referrals, by suburb, = 14 Armadale + 7 Kelmscott + 6 Seville Grove + 3 Mount Nasura + 2 Wungong + 2 Harrisdale + 2 Piara Waters.
  • The 24 new cases opened this month, by days to first contact, = 5 within 2 days + 11 at 3–7 days + 6 at 8–14 days + 2 over 14 days. 5 + 11 = 16 of 24, the 67% shown by the ring.
  • The centrepiece chart’s cases opened, March to August, = 18, 20, 21, 23, 24, 24; cases closed over the same months = 19, 18, 17, 18, 17, 17. August’s pair, 24 and 17, are the same figures as this month’s new cases opened and cases closed above.
  • Acceptance rate 67% is 24 ÷ 36, rounded to the nearest whole per cent.
  • The 3 referrals sitting under a non-canonical name for this team — 2 under the retired name, 1 under the misspelling — are counted in neither the 36 received above nor anywhere else on this page.

What this page does not know

This team does not yet record which of its own clinicians made a first contact, so first contact cannot be split by discipline on this page. That is different from a zero: a zero here means the count was taken and came back none; this means the count was never possible to take.

The 3 referrals sitting under a non-canonical name are known only because someone happened to notice them. This page cannot say whether other referrals are sitting under some other spelling nobody has flagged yet — the count of 3 is a floor, not a ceiling.

Not a duty roster, and not a performance target

  • The time-to-first-contact figures describe what happened, not what should happen. No target wait is drawn on this page, and a shorter median next month is not, by itself, evidence that anything changed for the better.
  • A zero anywhere on this page is a real, measured zero unless a caption beside it says the figure could not be taken. It is never used to mean “not tracked.”
  • Nothing on this page places a patient in a bed or accepts a referral on the team’s behalf. A decision made from a number here without a phone call to confirm it is a decision made on a convenience, not a source of truth.
