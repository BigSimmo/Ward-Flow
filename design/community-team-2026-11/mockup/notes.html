Mock-up notes

Rendered from the real Midland page in the local app, then rebuilt. Every person, clinician and number is invented.

What the app already holds

  • Team clients who are in a bed, via the referral the team accepted (the 9 names in In hospital)
  • Ward, days in bed and expected discharge date
  • Referrals sent to the team, with Accept and Decline
  • Discharge follow-up booked or not, and contact outcomes
  • Team phone, hours and address (invented, already labelled)

What it does not hold yet

  • The full caseload. Today it only knows people linked by a referral.
  • Case manager and treating psychiatrist for each client
  • Real clinician list, today's status, extensions and caseload limits (today's roster is a fixed sample)
  • Last seen and next appointment
  • CTO end and review dates (only the form is recorded)
  • Discharge date, so the 7-day follow-up clock can run
  • Which team sent a referral (most are unattributed)
  • For the full referral page: the handover text, risk summary, treatment and health, attached documents, client, carer, referrer and GP contacts, earlier episodes with the team, psychiatrist clinic slots, the team.s intake checks and team notes

A build would need

  • A caseload record per client with case manager, psychiatrist and dates, entered by the team or fed from the community record
  • A team staff list the team leader can edit
  • Your yes for the team to accept referrals as itself (today only Decline is offered on its page)
  • Documents on community referrals. Bed referrals can already carry charts and documents, so a build would reuse that.
