Decisions 10 OctOwner decisions, now in ward-flow-v9.css
  • Blue accent. One clear blue for anything you can press. Data bars stay slate.
  • Fixed hero light. One soft light at the top of every hero band. No clock light.
  • Visible glass. A cool tint, a stronger top sheen and a faint bottom shade, still about 80%.
  • Closed is slate. Closed beds and closed data take slate with a dashed edge, never gold.
  • Adopted parts. Quiet units, the mixed-face answer, fading hairline, PressureArc, figure roll, AllClear, BedPlan with pick up to place, RowSpark with delta twins, JourneyRibbon, Since you looked, the command palette and the Undo hairline.
  • Glare mode. data-mode="glare" on the root: solid surfaces, darker lines, heavier text. Try Glare in the top bar.
  • Parked. FlowMap, Departures board, hero fold, presence, Night shift mode and Wall mode. Not on this board.
